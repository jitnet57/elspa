# Vietnam Payroll System & Labor Law Compliance
**Date:** 2026-05-29  
**Regulatory Framework:** Vietnam Ministry of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs (MOLISA)  
**Currency:** Vietnamese Dong (VND) / USD equivalent  
**Tax Authority:** General Department of Taxation (GDT)

---

## Executive Summary

Vietnam's labor and payroll system requires strict compliance with mandatory social insurance contributions, progressive income tax withholding, and annual bonuses. The spa/massage industry has high labor intensity (60-75% of operating costs are personnel-related), making payroll efficiency critical. Key compliance requirements include BHXH (social insurance), BHYT (health insurance), and BHTN (unemployment insurance), with failure to comply incurring heavy penalties (up to 3x contribution amounts + potential closure).

---

## 1. Vietnamese Labor Law Overview

### 1.1 Regulatory Framework

| Regulation | Authority | Key Impact on Spa Business |
|------------|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Labor Code 2019** | MOLISA | Employment terms, wages, working hours, dismissal procedures |
| **Law on Social Insurance 2014 (amended 2020)** | MOLISA + Social Insurance Authority (SIA) | Mandatory BHXH contributions (employee + employer) |
| **Law on Health Insurance 2014** | Ministry of Health + SIA | Mandatory BHYT coverage for employees |
| **Law on Unemployment Insurance 2013** | MOLISA + SIA | BHTN contributions (employee + employer) |
| **Income Tax Law 2007 (amended 2022)** | GDT | Personal income tax (PIT) withholding (10% standard rate) |
| **Decree 90/2022** | Government | Increases minimum wage annually (effective Nov 1 each year) |
| **Decision 2345/2020-TTg** | Prime Minister | Mandatory 13th month bonus / annual benefits |

### 1.2 Employment Contract Requirements

**Mandatory Provisions:**
- Written contract (required for any employment > 3 months)
- Employee name, ID, address
- Job position and responsibilities
- Workplace location
- Salary amount and payment method
- Working hours and shift schedule
- Contract duration (fixed-term or indefinite)
- Insurance registration reference

**Contract Duration Options:**
1. **Trial Period:** Max 30 days (cannot exceed 10% of fixed-term contract length)
2. **Fixed-Term:** 12 months - 36 months (typical for seasonal workers in spas)
3. **Indefinite:** No end date (typical for senior staff)

**Probation & Trial:**
- Trial period for fixed-term contracts: up to 30 days
- Cannot withhold salary during trial
- Dismissal during trial requires written notice only (no penalty)

### 2.3 Compensation Components

**Base Salary:** Fixed monthly amount, must not be below regional minimum wage.

- Housing allowance: up to 30% of base salary
- Meal allowance: up to 10% of base salary
- Transportation allowance: up to 5% of base salary
- Uniform/clothing: up to 2% of base salary
- **Total allowances capped at 50% of base salary**

**Performance Bonus/Commission:**
- Tied to revenue targets, customer satisfaction scores, quality metrics
- Fully taxable
- Typical range: 5-30% of base salary for therapists

**Benefits (Tax-free):**
- Health insurance premium (employer contribution)
- Social insurance premium (employer contribution)
- Annual health check-up reimbursement (up to 5M VND)
- Training/professional development (up to 3M VND/year)

## 3. Mandatory Social Insurance & Health Insurance

### 3.1 BHXH (Social Insurance)

**Purpose:** Provides old-age (retirement), disability, and death benefits

**Contribution Rate (Monthly):**
| Payer | Rate | Calculation | Note |
|-------|------|-----------|------|
| **Employee** | 8% | 8% of gross salary | Withheld from paycheck |
| **Employer** | 17.5% | 17.5% of gross salary | Company pays |
| **Total** | 25.5% | Combined contribution | |

**Example (Therapist earning 8M VND/month):**
- Employee BHXH: 8M × 8% = 640,000 VND (withheld)
- Employer BHXH: 8M × 17.5% = 1,400,000 VND (company cost)
- Total: 2,040,000 VND

**Eligibility & Vesting:**
- Mandatory for all employees with employment contracts
- Contribution starts on day 1 of employment
- Minimum 20 years contribution required for full pension (age 60 for men, 55-60 for women)
- Early withdrawal: Not permitted (locked until retirement)

**Employer Responsibilities:**
- Register employee with Social Insurance Authority (SIA) within 30 days of hire
- Submit monthly contribution reports to SIA by 20th of following month
- Maintain records for 10 years

---

### 3.2 BHYT (Health Insurance)

**Purpose:** Covers employee medical expenses (hospitalization, outpatient care, medications)

**Contribution Rate:**
| Payer | Rate | Calculation |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **Employee** | 1.5% | 1.5% of gross salary |
| **Employer** | 3% | 3% of gross salary |
| **Total** | 4.5% | |

**Example (Therapist earning 8M VND/month):**
- Employee BHYT: 8M × 1.5% = 120,000 VND (withheld)
- Employer BHYT: 8M × 3% = 240,000 VND (company cost)
- Total: 360,000 VND

**Coverage & Benefits:**
- Employee + spouse + up to 2 children covered
- Covers 90% of approved medical costs
- Valid nationwide (portable)
- Must register at primary hospital/clinic for outpatient coverage

**Employer Responsibilities:**
- Enroll all employees within 30 days of hire
- Pay contributions monthly (due by 15th of following month)
- Provide insurance cards to employees
- Maintain enrollment records

---

### 3.3 BHTN (Unemployment Insurance)

**Purpose:** Provides income protection during involuntary unemployment and job transition support

**Contribution Rate:**
| Payer | Rate | Calculation |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **Employee** | 0.5% | 0.5% of gross salary |
| **Employer** | 0.5% | 0.5% of gross salary |
| **Total** | 1% | |

**Example (Therapist earning 8M VND/month):**
- Employee BHTN: 8M × 0.5% = 40,000 VND (withheld)
- Employer BHTN: 8M × 0.5% = 40,000 VND (company cost)
- Total: 80,000 VND

**Benefits & Eligibility:**
- Unemployed workers: 60-90% of average monthly salary for 3-12 months (based on contribution years)
- Minimum 12 months contributions to qualify
- Job seeking allowance (200K VND/month for 6 months)
- Skills training allowance (fully covered)

| Component | Employee Deduction | Employer Cost |

## 4. Personal Income Tax (PIT)

### 4.1 Progressive Tax Structure

Vietnam uses a **progressive 5-bracket system** (2022 Tax Law):

| Income Bracket (Monthly) | Tax Rate | Applicable to |
|-------------------------|----------|--------------|
| 0 - 5,000,000 VND | 5% | First 5M of gross income |
| 5,000,001 - 10,000,000 VND | 10% | Income portion between 5M-10M |
| 10,000,001 - 20,000,000 VND | 15% | Income portion between 10M-20M |
| 20,000,001 - 40,000,000 VND | 20% | Income portion between 20M-40M |
| 40,000,001+ VND | 25% | Income above 40M |

**Important Note:** This is marginal tax (bracket-based), not flat rate.

### 4.2 Tax Calculation Example

**Gross salary: 12,000,000 VND/month**

**Step 1: Calculate taxable income (after insurance deductions)**
- Gross: 12,000,000 VND
- BHXH deduction: 12M × 8% = 960,000 VND
- BHYT deduction: 12M × 1.5% = 180,000 VND
- BHTN deduction: 12M × 0.5% = 60,000 VND
- **Taxable income: 12M - 1.2M = 10,800,000 VND**

**Step 2: Apply progressive tax**
- First 5M @ 5%: 5M × 5% = 250,000 VND
- Next 5M @ 10%: 5M × 10% = 500,000 VND
- Remaining 800K @ 15%: 800K × 15% = 120,000 VND
- **Total PIT: 870,000 VND**

**Step 3: Calculate net pay**
- Taxable income: 10,800,000 VND
- PIT withholding: 870,000 VND
- **Net pay: 9,930,000 VND (or USD 405)**

### 4.3 Personal Income Tax Rates (Effective)

| Gross Monthly Salary | Effective PIT Rate | PIT Amount | Net Pay |
|---------------------|-------------------|-----------|---------|
| 6,000,000 VND | 4.1% | 240,000 | 5,340,000 |
| 8,000,000 VND | 5.3% | 400,000 | 7,140,000 |
| 10,000,000 VND | 6.3% | 560,000 | 8,875,000 |
| 15,000,000 VND | 7.8% | 1,035,000 | 13,395,000 |
| 20,000,000 VND | 8.9% | 1,605,000 | 17,925,000 |

### 4.4 Tax Withholding & Remittance

**Employer Responsibility:**
- Calculate and withhold PIT monthly from employee salaries
- Submit withheld PIT to tax authority by **10th of following month**
- Maintain payroll records and employee tax documents for **7 years**
- Issue annual tax certificate (Tờ Khai Thuế) to each employee

**Non-Tax Deductions:**
- Labor union fees (if applicable): 0.5-1% of salary
- Damage/breakage restitution: deductible only with employee written consent
- Court-ordered wage garnishment (child support, debt collection): deductible

## 5. Annual Bonuses & Benefits

### 5.1 13th Month Bonus (Mandatory)

**Regulation:** Decree 2345/2020-TTg (Prime Minister)

**Requirement:**
- Mandatory for all employees with at least 12 months employment
- Calculated based on average monthly salary
- Amount: **1 month base salary** (minimum)
- Timing: Must be paid before January 31 each year (for previous year performance)

**Calculation Example:**
- Average monthly base salary (Jan-Dec previous year): 8,000,000 VND
- 13th month bonus: 8,000,000 VND
- Tax treatment: Taxed as regular income (not bonus-specific deduction)

**Payment Options:**
1. Lump sum in December/January
2. Monthly distribution throughout the year (as long as total ≥ 1 month)
3. Combined with annual performance bonus (if performance-linked)

**Non-payment Penalties:**
- First offense: Fine 5-10M VND
- Second offense: Fine 10-20M VND
- Continued non-compliance: Business license suspension

### 5.2 Annual Performance/Merit Bonus

**Discretionary** (not mandated by law, but standard practice):
- Typical range: 0.5-3 months salary
- Tied to company performance, profitability, KPIs
- Negotiated in employment contract
- Fully taxable as income

### 5.3 Other Statutory Benefits

| Benefit | Provision | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Tet Bonus** | 1 month (some employers) | Customary but not mandatory |
| **Holiday Allowance** | Public holidays paid at regular rate | Mandatory (13 public holidays/year) |
| **Annual Leave Pay** | 100% of salary during annual leave | 12-14 days/year statutory minimum |
| **Sick Leave** | 100% pay for first 30 days/year | Doctor's cert required |
| **Maternity Leave** | 4 months (6+ months for 2+ children) | 100% salary during leave |
| **Paternity Leave** | 5-7 days | 100% salary |
| **Marriage Leave** | 3-5 days | 100% salary |
| **Bereavement Leave** | 1-3 days | 100% salary |

## 6. Working Hours & Overtime

### 6.1 Standard Working Hours

**Legal Standard:**
- **40 hours per week** (standard)
- **8 hours per day** (for 5-day week)
- **9 hours per day** maximum (for 4-day week)

**Spa Industry Practice (typical):**
- Most spas operate 10am-10pm (12-hour daily window)
- Therapists work rotating shifts: 6-8 hours/day
- On-call/standby staff common for walk-in demand
- Flexible scheduling to accommodate peak hours

### 6.2 Overtime Compensation

**Overtime Payment Rates (in addition to regular salary):**
| Overtime Type | Compensation | Notes |
|--------------|--------------|-------|
| **Weekday overtime** | 150% of hourly rate | Beyond 8 hrs/day |
| **Weekend work** | 200% of hourly rate | Saturday/Sunday regular work |
| **Holiday work** | 300% of hourly rate | Public holidays (13 days/year) |
| **Night shift (10pm-6am)** | 130% of hourly rate | Continuous night work bonus |

**Calculation Example:**
- Base hourly rate: 100,000 VND/hour (8M monthly ÷ 160 hours)
- Weekday overtime 2 hours: 2 hrs × 100K × 150% = 300,000 VND
- Weekend work 4 hours: 4 hrs × 100K × 200% = 800,000 VND
- Holiday work 8 hours: 8 hrs × 100K × 300% = 2,400,000 VND

**Overtime Limits:**
- Maximum 40 hours/month overtime
- Cannot exceed 20 days/year consecutive day shifts
- Employer must provide overtime voluntarily sign-off (not forced)
- Comp time alternative: Employee can request time-off instead of pay (at 150% rate value)

### 6.3 Rest & Leave Entitlements

**Annual Leave (Paid):**
- Minimum: 12 days/year (for <5 years service)
- Standard: 14 days/year (for 5+ years service)
- Senior staff: 16-18 days (negotiable)
- Unused leave: Paid out at end of employment (or may carry over per contract)

**Public Holidays (13 days/year, paid):**
- Tet Holiday: 7 consecutive days (usually Jan 29-Feb 4)
- Hung Kings' Day: 2 days (around April 18)
- Reunification Day: 1 day (April 30)
- International Labor Day: 1 day (May 1)
- National Day: 2 days (Sept 1-2)

## 7. Employment Termination & Severance

### 7.1 Termination Types

#### Employer Termination (Lay-Off / Dismissal)

**For-Cause Termination (No Severance):**
- Theft or dishonesty
- Willful misconduct
- Breach of employment contract
- Working for competitor during employment
- **Requires:** Written warning + 30-day cure period (except gross misconduct)

**Without-Cause Termination (With Severance):**
- Economic reasons (downsizing)
- Restructuring
- Unprofitable operations
- **Requires:** 30 days advance notice OR 1 month salary in lieu

**Severance Calculation:**
- **< 12 months service:** 50% of 1 month salary
- **12-36 months service:** 1 month salary
- **3+ years service:** 1 month salary + additional per year (varies)

#### Employee Termination (Resignation)

**With Notice:**
- 30 days advance notice required
- Written resignation letter
- No severance payable

**Immediate Resignation:**
- Acceptable only for serious breach by employer
- Requires written justification
- Employee may claim severance

**Termination Payment (Due on Last Day):**
- Remaining salary (pro-rated if mid-month)
- Accrued unused annual leave (at 100% rate)
- 13th month bonus (pro-rated if <12 months)
- Severance (if applicable)
- All deductions for overpayment or damages (with limits)

### 7.2 Severance Examples (Spa Therapist)

**Scenario 1: Employer terminates mid-level therapist (8M/month salary) after 18 months**
- Base severance: 8M × 1 month = 8,000,000 VND
- 13th month bonus (pro-rated 18/12): 12,000,000 VND
- Unused annual leave (10 days × 8M ÷ 22 days): 3,636,364 VND
- **Total severance: 23,636,364 VND (USD 965)**

**Scenario 2: Employer terminates senior therapist (15M/month) after 5 years (30 days notice)**
- Base severance: 15M × 2 months = 30,000,000 VND
- 13th month bonus (full year): 15,000,000 VND
- Unused annual leave (12 days × 15M ÷ 22): 8,181,818 VND
- Final month salary (partial): Pro-rated
- **Total severance: 53,181,818 VND (USD 2,170)**

## 8. Compliance & Penalty Framework

### 8.1 Payroll Compliance Checklist

**Employee Registration & Contracts:**
- [ ] Written employment contracts for all staff (>3 months)
- [ ] Contracts contain all mandatory provisions
- [ ] Employee ID cards verified and copied
- [ ] Employee tax registration ID (PIT ID) recorded

**Social Insurance (BHXH):**
- [ ] All employees registered with SIA within 30 days of hire
- [ ] Monthly BHXH contributions calculated correctly (8% employee + 17.5% employer)
- [ ] Contributions submitted to SIA by 20th of following month
- [ ] Records retained for 10 years

**Health Insurance (BHYT):**
- [ ] All employees enrolled in health insurance
- [ ] Monthly BHYT contributions (1.5% employee + 3% employer)
- [ ] Contributions submitted by 15th of following month
- [ ] Insurance cards issued and distributed

**Unemployment Insurance (BHTN):**
- [ ] BHTN contributions (0.5% employee + 0.5% employer) deducted monthly
- [ ] Contributions submitted with BHXH/BHYT

**Income Tax (PIT):**
- [ ] PIT calculated correctly per progressive brackets
- [ ] Taxes withheld from employee paychecks
- [ ] Monthly tax remittance to GDT by 10th of following month
- [ ] Annual tax reconciliation (January-March)

**Bonuses & Benefits:**
- [ ] 13th month bonus paid before Jan 31 each year
- [ ] Performance bonuses documented and justified
- [ ] Annual leave accrual tracked and paid out
- [ ] Overtime compensation calculated and recorded

**Payroll Records:**
- [ ] Monthly payroll registers maintained
- [ ] Attendance/time-tracking system (manual or digital)
- [ ] Payment method documented (bank, cash)
- [ ] Employee signatures on payroll confirmations
- [ ] 7-year retention for all records

### 8.2 Penalty Structure

**Late or Incomplete Insurance Contributions:**
| Violation | Penalty | Cumulative Risk |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **1-30 days late** | 0.1% of unpaid amount per day | Up to 3% on contributions |
| **30-60 days late** | 0.2% of unpaid amount per day | Up to 6% |
| **60+ days late** | 0.3% of unpaid amount per day | Up to 9% + interest |
| **Non-reporting** | 5-10M VND fine | Business interruption risk |

**Example:**
- BHXH due: 2,000,000 VND (10 employees)
- Payment 45 days late
- Penalty: 2M × 45 days × 0.2% = 180,000 VND (plus interest)

**Non-Payment or False Declaration:**
| Offense | Penalty | Consequence |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Missing 13th month bonus** | 5-20M VND fine | Reputational damage |
| **Wage theft / under-payment** | 3-10x unpaid amount + fine | Forced repayment + business suspension |
| **No employment contract** | 10-20M VND per employee | Staff turnover risk |
| **Failure to comply with working hours** | 15-30M VND + corrective action | Labor inspection ongoing |
| **Ignoring labor inspection** | 20-50M VND + business license suspension | Operational shutdown |

**Criminal Liability:**
- Repeated violations (3+ instances) may trigger criminal charges
- Personal liability for business owners/managers
- Potential jail time for serious violations (0.5-3 years)

### 8.3 Labor Inspections

**Frequency & Scope:**
- Random inspections: Quarterly in high-risk sectors (hospitality, spa, security)
- Complaint-based inspections: Within 30 days of filing
- **Inspection Coverage:** Contracts, wages, working hours, insurance, safety

**Inspection Process:**
1. Inspectors arrive with notification order
2. Document review (contracts, payroll, records)
3. Employee interviews (confidential)
4. On-site observations (hours, conditions)
5. Written report + recommendations (30 days)
6. Corrective action plan due (60 days from report)

**High-Risk Inspection Triggers:**
- Employee complaints to labor authorities
- Media reports of wage theft
- Social media allegations
- Turnover spike (suggesting labor issues)
- Changes in business registration
