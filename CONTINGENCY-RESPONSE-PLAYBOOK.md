## 📌 OVERVIEW

**Purpose:** Define decision trees + escalation paths for major contingencies  
**Activation:** When planned metric deviates >20% from target

**Communication:** Immediate Slack alert + standup decision

## CONTINGENCY #1: Board Meeting Postponed (Pre-Launch)

### **Trigger:** Board postpones May 30 approval meeting

**Impact:** Delays strategic sign-off + budget approval

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
Board meeting postponed to [new date]?

├─ YES, same week (May 31-Jun 2)
│  └─ ACTION A1: Start Week 1 execution WITHOUT board sign-off
│     ├─ CEO personal fund approval (if needed)
│     ├─ Proceed with 3x tracks as planned
│     ├─ Brief board async (Slack/email) on progress
│     └─ Full board debrief on actual results (post-launch)
│
├─ YES, following week (Jun 8+)
│  └─ ACTION A2: Proceed with Week 1, present results as proof-of-concept
│     ├─ Execute all 3 tracks (Week 1 as designed)
│     ├─ Collect metrics: meetings, demos, NPS
│     ├─ Board meeting Jun 8: Present Week 1 RESULTS (not plan)
│     ├─ Decision: Scale Week 2 based on actual data
│     └─ Budget approval post-data (more convincing)
│
└─ UNCLEAR: Board silent, no confirmation
   └─ ACTION A3: CEO makes executive decision
      ├─ Call board chair (30-min conversation)
      ├─ Present cost/benefit: "If we wait, we lose momentum"
      ├─ Get verbal approval OR proceed anyway
      └─ Document decision in Slack #week1-execution
```

---

### **Action A1: Launch Without Board Sign-Off**

**Decision Time:** May 30, 14:00 (if board silence confirmed)

**Immediate Actions:**
1. **CEO:** Record a 5-min video explaining Week 1 plan + contingency rationale
2. **CEO:** Send to board chair + CFO (async approval)
3. **PM:** Begin Week 1 execution Friday morning (full go)
4. **CS:** Prepare daily metrics digest for board (to show progress)
5. **All:** Document all decisions + costs in separate "contingency log"

**Budget Workaround (if needed):**
- Use existing Q2 operational budget (no new approval needed)
- Reclassify as "customer acquisition pilot" (standard business category)
- Document actual spend vs. plan in weekly report

**Success Criteria:**
- Week 1 metrics prove concept (3+ partner meetings + 10+ demos + 50 NPS)
- Board approves Week 2 scale-up (by Jun 8)

## CONTINGENCY #2: Partner Unresponsive (Track A)

### **Trigger:** 3+ partners silent after 48 hours (by Mon 17:00)

**Impact:** Missing meetings → Lower partner pipeline

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
3+ partners still unresponsive by Monday 17:00?

├─ YES (all email + LinkedIn + 1 phone call attempted)
│  └─ DECISION B1: Pivot to new partner list
│     ├─ Rationale: These partners may not be good fit
│     ├─ Action: CEO identifies 5 NEW partners (alternate list)
│     ├─ Execution: Email new list Tue morning (expedited)
│     └─ Target: 1-2 meetings by Thu (adjusted from 3)
│
├─ PARTIAL (1-2 silent, 1-2 responding)
│  └─ DECISION B2: Continue escalation on responders + move silent to nurture
│     ├─ Silent partners: Send 6-month nurture sequence (automated)
│     ├─ Responding partners: Accelerate to meeting
│     └─ Target: 2 meetings (vs. 3) by Thu
│
└─ VERY BAD (all 5 silent + no response)
   └─ DECISION B3: Pivot to conference + warm referral strategy
      ├─ CEO: Work LinkedIn network for warm intros (1-2 partners)
      ├─ PM: Find 3 partner company names from industry news
      ├─ Strategy: Warm intro-first (no cold email)
      └─ Timeline: New partner meetings likely Week 2 (not Week 1)
```

---

### **Action B1: Pivot to New Partner List (If 3+ Silent)**

**Activation Time:** Monday 15:00 (decision call)

**Process (Mon 15:00-17:00):**

1. **CEO:** Review alternate partner list (pre-prepared backup)
   - Criteria: Similar to original 5 (50+ employees, therapist ops)
   - Examples: TherapyGrowth, ZenWellness, HealthScale
   - Validation: Confirm contact emails + phone #

2. **Email new list (Tue 08:00):**
   - Same email template as original 5
   - Slight variation: "Saw your company expanded to..."
   - Target: Send to 5 new partners by 08:30

3. **Escalation schedule:**
   - Tue 18:00: Check for responses (expect 24-48h response time)
   - Wed 09:00: LinkedIn + phone call to non-responders
   - Thu 17:00: Debrief (target: 1+ new meetings)

**Adjusted Week 1 Target (if executing B1):**
- Original: 3 partner meetings
- Adjusted: 2 partner meetings (1 from original list + 1 from new list)
- Backup: 0 meetings (proceed to Week 2 partner push)

---

### **Action B2: Partial Response (1-2 Responding)**

**Process (Mon 17:00):**

1. **Hot list (responding partners):**
   - Accelerate scheduling: Aim for Tue-Wed demos
   - Preparation: Have product lead ready for fast turnaround

2. **Cold list (silent partners):**
   - Add to 6-month nurture sequence (auto emails)
   - Rationale: They're interested but not urgent
   - Timeline: Re-approach in Q3 when company scales

3. **Metrics adjustment:**
   - Lower Week 1 target from 3 → 2 meetings
   - Reclassify as "success" if 2+ meetings achieved

---

### **Action B3: All Silent (Nuclear Option)**

**Activation:** If all 5 partners silent by Mon 17:00 + no responses to escalation

**Pivot Strategy:**

1. **Acknowledge reality:** Cold email campaigns have ~5-20% response rate
   - 5 partners = expected 0-1 responses (statistically normal)
   - This is NOT a failure (just variance)

2. **Shift strategy (Tue AM):**
   - CEO works LinkedIn network directly
   - Find 2-3 warm intros from existing connections
   - Warm intro = 50% higher response rate
   - Timeline: Meetings likely Week 2 (not Week 1)

3. **Week 1 decision:**
   - Track A lowers target from 3 → 0 meetings (call it a learning week)
   - Focus energy on Track B (customer acquisition)
   - Re-prioritize Week 2 for partner outreach (warm + referral strategy)

4. **Communication (Mon 17:00):**

Slack update:

```
🔴 CONTINGENCY B3 ACTIVATED: Partner Response Rate Low

Situation: 5 cold emails sent Fri, 0 responses by Mon 17:00
This is statistically normal (industry avg ~10% response rate)

Decision: Pivot to warm intro strategy for Week 2
- CEO working LinkedIn for 2-3 warm intros (Tue-Wed)
- Expected response rate: 50%+ (much better than cold)
- Week 1 target: Lower from 3 → 0 meetings
- Week 2 target: 3+ meetings (warm strategy)

Benefit: Warmer partnerships are higher quality anyway

Track B (customers) + Track C (NPS) proceeding as planned
```

## CONTINGENCY #3: Cold Call Low Conversion (Track B)

### **Trigger:** <15% demo conversion rate by Mid-Tuesday (after 30 calls)

**Impact:** Missing customer acquisition targets

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
Demo conversion rate <15% by Tue 14:00 (after 30 calls)?

├─ YES (only 4-5 demos from 30 calls)
│  └─ DECISION C1: Pause + Script Audit
│     ├─ Review call recordings (best 3 + worst 3)
│     ├─ Identify blocker: objection handling? Pitch clarity? Wrong list?
│     ├─ Adjust script by Tue 15:00
│     ├─ A/B test new script (5 test calls Tue 16:00-17:00)
│     └─ Deploy new script Wed morning (if improved)
│
├─ MEDIUM (15-20% conversion)
│  └─ DECISION C2: Script is OK, maybe LIST issue
│     ├─ Review customer list: Are fit scores accurate?
│     ├─ Option 1: Rerank list (promote higher-fit customers)
│     ├─ Option 2: Add backup list (new prospects, different industry)
│     └─ Continue with improvements, monitor conversion daily
│
└─ OK RANGE (20%+)
   └─ DECISION C3: On track
      ├─ No changes needed
      ├─ Maintain current approach
      └─ Monitor for objections + iterate
```

---

### **Action C1: Script Pause & Audit**

**Trigger Time:** Tue 14:00 (after 30 calls)

**Audit Process (Tue 14:00-15:00):**

1. **PM reviews call recordings:**
   - Best 3 calls (demos scheduled): What worked?
   - Worst 3 calls (rejections): What failed?

2. **Identify pattern:**
   - Pitch too long? (cut to 30 sec)
   - Objection response weak? (improve 3 main responses)
   - Qualification step missing? (ask "do you use therapists?" earlier)
   - Wrong timing? (call during business hours vs. breaks)

3. **Case example:**

**WORST CALL:**
```
Rep: "Hi Sarah, this is [name] from ElSpa..."
Sarah: "Not interested" [Click]

Problem: Didn't hook attention. Jumped to pitch without permission.
```

**REVISED SCRIPT:**
```
Rep: "Hi Sarah, quick question - are you currently managing 
therapists at [Company]?"
Sarah: "Yes, why?"
Rep: "Perfect. We just built something that might save you 
5+ hours a week on scheduling. Free 15-min call this week?"
```

4. **A/B test new script (Tue 16:00-17:00):**
   - 5 calls with revised script
   - Track conversion rate
   - If improved: Deploy to team Wed
   - If same: Try different angle

---

### **Action C2: List Quality Issue**

**Process (Tue 14:00):**

1. **Rerank customer list:**
   - Pull fit scores for all 30 called-so-far
   - Average fit score: 3.2? (should be 3.5+)
   - If low: Remove bottom 10, insert backup list

2. **Backup list strategy:**
   - Pre-prepare 20 alternate prospects (lower fit, but fresh)
   - Replace low-fit customers with these alternates
   - Continue calls Wed with better-fit prospects

3. **Objection pattern:**
   - If hearing "not relevant to us": List is too broad
   - If hearing "too expensive": List is industry mismatch
   - Adjust targeting accordingly

---

### **Action C1 Script Revision Example**

**Problem:** "Using competitor" objection (30% of rejections)

**Old Response:**
```
"Oh, we're different from [competitor]. We have [features]..."
[Prospect hangs up]
```

**New Response (A):**
```
"Totally get it. Most people are happy with [competitor] until 
they try us. One quick difference we offer is [specific feature]. 
Worth 10 min to see the difference?"
```

**New Response (B):**
```
"That's great! [Competitor] does X well. We're actually better 
at Y and Z. Since you already have scheduling, what's missing?"
[Listen for gap]
```

**New Response (C):**
```
"Cool. Quick question - is [feature] something you struggle with?"
[If YES] "That's exactly what we solve. 15-min call this week?"
[If NO] "No worries. I'll stay on your radar for next year."
```

---

**Tue 16:00-17:00 (Test window):**

```

| Call # | Old Script? | New Script? | Demo? | Objection |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|-----------|
```

- Try completely different angle (pain-first vs. benefit-first)
- Or abandon cold calling entirely (pivot to referral-based)

## CONTINGENCY #5: Major Objection Pattern (Track B)

### **Trigger:** Same objection in >50% of calls (e.g., "too expensive")

**Impact:** Indicates product-market fit issue OR pricing problem

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
Same objection appearing in >50% of calls?

├─ YES - "Too expensive" / "Budget constraint"
│  └─ DECISION E1: Introduce discount or ROI discussion
│     ├─ Option A: Offer 20% discount (limited time)
│     ├─ Option B: Shift to ROI: "What would you save?"
│     ├─ Option C: Offer free trial (no payment required)
│     └─ Test which resonates (A/B test with reps)
│
├─ YES - "Using [competitor] already"
│  └─ DECISION E2: Differentiation + Migration offer
│     ├─ Identify what [competitor] DOESN'T do
│     ├─ New pitch: "[Competitor] + our feature = 2x value"
│     ├─ Offer: Free migration help
│     └─ Revised objection response (see earlier template)
│
├─ YES - "Too busy right now" / "Call back later"
│  └─ DECISION E3: Lower friction close
│     ├─ Don't ask for "demo this week"
│     ├─ Instead: "When is a better time? Pin down specific date"
│     ├─ Or: "Want me to send a 2-min video instead of call?"
│     └─ Revised close: Async options instead of live calls
│
└─ ONGOING PATTERN (month+)
   └─ DECISION E4: Product-market fit issue
      ├─ Rationale: If 50%+ rejection for same reason,
      │             maybe product doesn't address key pain
      ├─ Action: Pivot product messaging (emphasize different benefit)
      ├─ Or: Pivot target customer (different industry)
      └─ Week 2 planning: Major strategy review needed
```

---

### **Action E1: Discount Introduction (If "Too Expensive")**

**When to activate:** >50% of calls (Tue 14:00 or earlier)

**Communication to team (Tue 14:30):**

Slack message:

```
🎯 OBJECTION PATTERN ALERT: "Too expensive" (50%+ of calls)

New script approved for objection handling:

OLD: "Our pricing is fair for the value..."
NEW: "I get budget concerns. Here's what we can do:
     - First 3 months at 30% discount (early adopter rate)
     - Or: Pilot program (1 location only, lower cost)
     Which interests you more?"

Start using new response immediately.
If they say "still too much": Move to ROI calculator.

Questions? Slack @PM
```

**ROI Calculator Backup Script:**

```
"Fair question. Let me flip it - how much time per month 
do you currently spend on therapist scheduling?"

[They respond: "5 hours", "10 hours", etc.]

"OK so at $X/hour, that's $Y/month in labor cost. 
Our platform eliminates that. ROI is usually 8 weeks. 
Make sense?"

[They respond YES/NO]

"Great. Let's do a free trial and measure your actual time saved. 
Start next week?"
```

---

### **Action E3: Async Close (If "Too Busy")**

**Problem:** 20%+ saying "too busy now"

**New Close (revised script):**

```
OLD: "I have these times available this week..."
NEW: "I totally get being slammed. How about I send you a 
     2-minute video overview first? Then we can talk next week?"

[OR]

NEW: "When's a better time? I can call you 6/15 at 2pm?"
[Pin down SPECIFIC future time - not vague]

[OR]

NEW: "What if I just send you a Calendly link and you 
     pick a time that works? No pressure."
```

**Expected Result:** More demos scheduled (further out, but confirmed)

---

### **Action E4: Product Pivot Assessment**

**Trigger:** If "too expensive" objection continues for >1 week

**Assessment (Week 2, Mon):**

**Question:** Is pricing actually the issue, or is it...?

```
Diagnosis questions:
1. Do they understand what we do?
   → If no: Change pitch message
2. Do they see value?
   → If no: Different pain point?
3. Do they want to buy?
   → If no: Not a good fit customer

If pattern is "don't see value" (not pricing):
→ Pivot message to different benefit
→ Try different target customer

If pattern is "good fit but price point too high":
→ Accept that early adopters need discount
→ Plan for price increase in Month 3 (after traction)
```

## CONTINGENCY #6: Week 1 Disaster (Multiple Failures)

### **Trigger:** All 3 tracks underperform (0 meetings + 0 demos + <30 NPS responses)

**Impact:** Complete reboot needed

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
Multiple tracks failing by Wed?
(Track A: 0 meetings, Track B: <5 demos, Track C: <30 responses)

├─ YES (complete failure)
│  └─ DECISION F1: Pivot to SURVIVAL MODE
│     ├─ CEO: Analyze root cause (technical? market? timing?)
│     ├─ Hold all-hands (Wed 15:00, 1 hour)
│     ├─ Revisit fundamental assumptions
│     ├─ Decide: Continue Week 1 or pivot entirely
│     ├─ If pivot: New Week 1 strategy (Thu morning start)
│     └─ If continue: Intensive deep-dive (why failing)
│
└─ PARTIAL (1-2 tracks failing, 1 strong)
   └─ DECISION F2: Double down on winning track
      ├─ Reallocate resources from weak → strong track
      ├─ Use strong track momentum to support weak tracks
      └─ Asymmetric strategy (not balanced 3-track)
```

---

### **Action F1: Survival Mode Activation (If Disaster)**

**All-Hands Meeting (Wed 15:00, 1 hour):**

**Agenda:**

1. **CEO Diagnosis (15 min):**
   - What went wrong? Be specific.
   - Customer feedback (not our assumptions)?
   - Product issue? Market issue? Timing?

2. **Root Cause Analysis (20 min):**
   - Track A: Why no partner responses?
   - Track B: Why no customer demos?
   - Track C: Why low NPS engagement?

3. **Decision: Pivot or Persist? (15 min):**
   - Is fundamental strategy wrong?
   - Or is execution just slow?
   - CEO makes call: Continue Week 1 OR restart Week 1

4. **New Plan (10 min):**
   - If restart: New approach Thu morning
   - If continue: Intensive Wed-Thu push

---

### **Restart Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1: Wrong Product-Market Fit**

```
Symptoms:
- Customers say "don't see value"
- Partners uninterested in partnership
- NPS feedback: "Not relevant to us"

Action: Pivot product messaging
- Maybe it's not "therapist scheduling"
- Maybe it's "wellness team management" or "staff efficiency"
- Re-position same product, different benefit

Timeline: New pitch Thu AM, restart calls Thu 10:00
```

**Scenario 2: Wrong Customer Segment**

```
Symptoms:
- Healthcare companies interested, beauty companies not
- Only large companies responding, SMBs ignoring
- Geographic pattern (Asia vs. US mismatch)

Action: Pivot target customer
- Narrow to best-fit segment (e.g., healthcare only)
- Find new prospect list in that vertical
- Restart Thu with focused list

Timeline: List creation Wed 17:00-18:00, calls Thu 10:00
```

**Scenario 3: Execution Issue (Not Strategy)**

```
Symptoms:
- Some early interest (not zero)
- Feedback unclear / mixed
- Team energy low

Action: Intensive execution sprint
- Improve script based on early feedback
- CEO makes some calls personally (model success)
- Team morale building + reset expectations

Timeline: Thursday AM recovery, reset targets for Fri
```

---

### **Action F2: Double-Down Strategy (Partial Failure)**

**Example: Track A strong (2 meetings), Track B weak (2 demos)**

**Reallocation (Wed 16:00):**

1. **PM partially shifts to Track A support:**
   - Help CEO prep for 2 partner demos (Tue-Wed)
   - Ensure strong outcomes (= momentum for Track B)

2. **Sales team doubling down on Track B:**
   - More calls per rep (4 calls/hour instead of 3)
   - Extended window (17:00-18:00 buffer)
   - Fresh list of prospects (PM provides)

3. **CS stays on Track C:**
   - Continue NPS survey + outreach
   - May see lift from partner meetings (referrals)

**Rationale:** Capitalize on wins (Track A) to generate momentum for struggles (Track B)

## CONTINGENCY #7: Key Team Member Unavailable

### **Trigger:** CEO, PM, or CS Lead suddenly unavailable (illness, emergency)

**Impact:** Track loses owner, execution stalls

---

### **Decision Tree:**

```
Key person unavailable during Week 1?

├─ CEO UNAVAILABLE
│  └─ DECISION G1: PM becomes acting CEO
│     ├─ PM runs daily standups
│     ├─ PM makes final decisions on pivots
│     ├─ CS Lead doubles as interim Track C lead
│     ├─ Sales reps auto-escalate to PM
│     └─ Expected timeline: 2-5 days recovery
│
├─ PM UNAVAILABLE
│  └─ DECISION G2: Sales rep #1 takes Track B
│     ├─ CEO oversees Track B decisions
│     ├─ Rep #1 keeps detailed call logs
│     ├─ CEO checks in 2x daily (09:00, 17:00)
│     └─ Expected timeline: 3-7 days recovery
│
└─ CS LEAD UNAVAILABLE
   └─ DECISION G3: PM assumes Track C
      ├─ CEO oversees NPS responses
      ├─ Focus: NPS <6 response only (not nice-to-haves)
      ├─ Auto-responder email from CS email (explains delay)
      └─ Expected timeline: 2-4 days recovery
```

---

### **Action G1: PM Acting CEO**

**Assumption of duties (immediate):**

1. **Slack announcement (1 min after notification):**
   ```
   @channel: CEO [Name] unexpectedly unavailable this week.
   PM [Name] assuming command for Week 1 launch.
   No changes to plan - full execution continues.
   Stand by for normal 09:00 standup.
   ```

2. **PM's new responsibilities:**
   - Run 09:00, 14:00, 17:00 standups (same format)
   - Make day-to-day decisions (script changes, list pivots)
   - Escalate major decisions to board via Slack (async approval)
   - Check in 1:1 with CEO daily (15 min status call)

3. **Track B continuity:**
   - Delegate Track B to senior sales rep
   - Rep reports to PM (instead of CEO)
   - CEO still owns Track A remotely (if condition permits)
