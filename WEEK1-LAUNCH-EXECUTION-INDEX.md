### **4. CONTINGENCY-RESPONSE-PLAYBOOK.md** (27 KB)
**Purpose:** Emergency protocols + decision trees for major contingencies

**Contains:**
- **Contingency #1:** Board meeting postponed
  - Actions: Launch without approval | Async brief board | Executive decision
  
- **Contingency #2:** Partner unresponsive (3+ silent)
  - Actions: Pivot to new list | Partial escalation | Nuclear LinkedIn strategy
  
- **Contingency #3:** Cold call low conversion (<15%)
  - Actions: Script audit | A/B testing | List quality review | Phone blitz
  
- **Contingency #4:** NPS survey low response (<40%)
  - Actions: SMS reminder | CEO personal email | Phone follow-up | Incentive increase
  
- **Contingency #5:** Major objection pattern (>50% same objection)
  - Actions: Discount intro | ROI calculation | Differentiation | Async close
  
- **Contingency #6:** Week 1 disaster (multiple failures)
  - Actions: Root cause analysis | Restart vs. persist | Survival mode
  
- **Contingency #7:** Key person unavailable
  - Actions: Acting roles | Decision delegation | Remote management

**Decision Authority:**
- Green (minor): Track owner self-approval (<1h)
- Yellow (medium): CEO approval (2h)
- Orange (major): CEO + CFO (4h)
- Red (critical): CEO + Board (8h)

## 🎯 KEY SUCCESS FACTORS

### **Track A (Partner Outreach):**
1. Personalization in every email (mention their specific context)
2. Speed (respond to positive signal within 1 hour)
3. Persistence (LinkedIn + phone escalation by Day 3)

### **Track B (Customer Calling):**
1. Energy (confident tone = 80% of success)
2. Listening (diagnose pain before pitching)
3. Consistency (3 calls/hour × 8 hours = 24 calls/day)

### **Track C (NPS Management):**
1. Rapid response (phone calls within 4 hours for at-risk)
2. Solutions (offer 2-3 options, not just acknowledgment)
3. Follow-through (implement solutions, track improvement)
