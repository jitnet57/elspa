### 1.3 Cebu Expansion Economics & Unit Economics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Model:**

| Channel | Cost/Customer | Conversion Rate | Effort | Payback (months) |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Founder Direct** | PHP 2K | 10-15% | High (time) | 8-12 months |
| **Referral Partner** | PHP 1-2K | 30%+ | Low (commission) | 6-10 months |
| **Content/Inbound** | PHP 5K | 5% | High (creation) | 20-24 months |
| **Trade Show** | PHP 2-3K | 5-10% | Medium | 12-18 months |

**Blended CAC (Phase 1B):** ~PHP 1.5K per customer (at scale, with partners)

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):**

**Base Calculation:**
- Average Spa Size: 25 therapists
- Average Monthly Payroll: PHP 600K
- ElSpa Price: PHP 2.5K/month (blended, based on therapist count)
- Gross Margin: 80% (SaaS model)
- Customer Churn Rate: 10% annual (5% annual, conservatively)
- **LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate = (PHP 2.5K × 0.8) / 0.05 = PHP 40K**

**LTV/CAC Ratio:** PHP 40K / PHP 1.5K = 26.7x (excellent; target > 3x)

**Payback Period:** 6-8 months (CAC recovered; then profitable)

**Cohort Analysis (Customer Retention):**

| Cohort | Month 1 | Month 6 | Month 12 | Churn Rate |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Apr 2027** | 100% | 95% | 90% | ~10% annually |
| **Jun 2027** | 100% | 94% | 88% | ~12% annually |
| **Aug 2027** | 100% | 92% | - | (early stage) |

**Insight:** 88-90% annual retention = sustainable growth (industry benchmark: 85%+)

## Part 2: Bohol Expansion Strategy

### 2.1 Bohol Market Analysis & Rationale

**Why Bohol? (Strategic Advantages)**

| Factor | Benefit |
|--------|---------|
| **Geographic Proximity** | 1-hour ferry to Cebu (operational leverage) |
| **Competitive Vacuum** | Only 2-3 established spas; 95% manual payroll (same pain as Cebu) |
| **Growth Rate** | 15-20% CAGR vs. 12% Cebu (faster market expansion) |
| **Lower Costs** | 40-50% lower rent, wages (allows aggressive pricing) |
| **Untapped Tourism** | 450K annual visitors; high willingness to pay |
| **First-Mover Advantage** | No competitor has Bohol foothold yet |
| **Lessons Learned** | Can apply Cebu playbook + customizations |

**Bohol Spa Market Overview (2026):**

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Total Spas/Salons** | 40-50 | Much smaller than Cebu |
| **Addressable (15-50 therapists)** | 25-30 | Mostly mid-size, tourist-focused |
| **Total Therapists (addressable)** | 550-700 | Lower therapist density |
| **Monthly Payroll Volume** | PHP 13-17M | 550-700 × PHP 24K avg |
| **Current Payroll Method** | 95% manual/Excel | Accountant-dependent |
| **Addressable TAM** | PHP 39-51M | 30% of payroll (compliance burden) |

**Tourist-Heavy Dynamic (vs. Cebu):**
- 60%+ of spas in Panglao, Alona Beach (resort-adjacent)
- High seasonal volatility (50% more variability than Cebu)
- Premium pricing (tourists pay 20-30% more)
- Lower local recurring customer base
- **Implication:** Payroll software needs off-season flexibility (seasonal staffing)

### 2.3 Bohol vs. Cebu Operating Model

**Same Platform, Different Markets:**

| Aspect | Cebu | Bohol |
|--------|------|-------|
| **Product Base** | Standard payroll | + Seasonal payroll feature |
| **Pricing** | PHP 2-3K/month (tiered) | PHP 2-3K/month (same; opaque volume) |
| **Support Language** | Tagalog, English | Tagalog primary |
| **Payment Terms** | Monthly (recurring) | Monthly + seasonal adjustments |
| **Key Partnership** | Accountants, suppliers | Resorts, tourism council |
| **Customer Support** | Founder + 1 FTE | Part-time local person (outsourced) |
| **Revenue Reporting** | Integrated (Cebu + Bohol combined) | Separate P&L for resorts/spas |

**Shared Infrastructure:**
- ✅ Same SaaS database (Bohol = different region/tenant)
- ✅ Same BIR compliance engine (all Bohol spas must file same forms)
- ✅ Same payroll calculation (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG identical)
- ✅ Shared operations (customer onboarding, billing, support)

**Bohol-Specific Customizations:**
- ✅ Seasonal payroll feature (on/off staffing per month)
- ✅ Tourism pricing tier (higher margin for resort white-label)
- ✅ Regional marketing assets
- ✅ Local partnership network

**Cost Synergies:**
- Development: Amortized across 2 regions (lower per-region R&D)
- Operations: Single team serves both markets (leverage)
- Marketing: Shared content, adapted for region
- Support: Partial outsourcing to Bohol (cost savings vs. Manila)

## Part 7: Risk Management & Contingencies

### 7.1 Key Risks & Mitigation

**Risk 1: Slow Customer Acquisition**
- **Trigger:** Only 5 customers by end Q2 2027 (below Phase 1A goal)
- **Probability:** Medium (30%)
- **Impact:** Revenue miss, team morale
- **Mitigation:**
  - Pivot to accountant-first channel (direct outreach to 50+ accountants)
  - Increase founder travel budget (weekly in-person visits)
  - Launch aggressive promotion (month 1 free, 50% discount)
  - Tighten sales process (video + trial → conversion)

**Risk 2: Product Gaps (Compliance Errors)**
- **Trigger:** BIR audit catches ElSpa client with incorrect withholding
- **Probability:** Low (10%)
- **Impact:** Customer lawsuit, brand damage, regulatory scrutiny
- **Mitigation:**
  - Formal compliance review (hire external auditor Q1 2027)
  - Test payroll against 10+ real scenarios (edge cases)
  - Employee insurance (E&O policy)
  - Rapid response protocol (customer support, BIR notification)

**Risk 3: Competitor Entry (HRPlus adds spa features)**
- **Trigger:** HRPlus launches "Spa Payroll" module with PHP 1.5K pricing
- **Probability:** Medium (20% in Year 1, 50% in Year 2)
- **Impact:** Price pressure, reduced differentiation
- **Mitigation:**
  - Build moat faster (integrations, benchmarking data)
  - Lock-in via long-term contracts (annual pre-pay discounts)
  - Franchise strategy (volume customer = sticky)
  - Customer success (high NPS = retention even if price undercut)

**Risk 4: Seasonality Volatility (Bohol revenue swings)**
- **Trigger:** June-September low season = 40-50% revenue drop
- **Probability:** High (100% - predictable)
- **Impact:** Cash flow challenges, team retention pressure
- **Mitigation:**
  - Seasonal customer mix (50%+ local, 50% tourist) = smoothing
  - Annual pre-pay discounts (cash up-front)
  - Resort white-label contracts (stable, year-round)
  - Cebu growth offsets Bohol seasonality (larger, more stable base)

**Risk 5: BIR Policy Changes**
- **Trigger:** New tax law, SSS rate change, PhilHealth premium adjustment
- **Probability:** Medium (30% annually)
- **Impact:** Feature updates required, compliance uncertainty
- **Mitigation:**
  - Modular payroll calculation (quick updates)
  - BIR monitoring (subscribe to tax alerts)
  - Rapid response team (72-hour update target)
  - Customer notification (proactive communication)

### 7.2 Financial Contingencies

**Conservative Scenario (70% customer projections):**
- Year 1 (2027): 50 customers, PHP 1.5M ARR, PHP 800K EBITDA
- Still profitable; extended payback period

**Optimistic Scenario (130% customer projections):**
- Year 1 (2027): 90 customers, PHP 2.7M ARR, PHP 1.6M EBITDA
- Justifies larger team hire, accelerated Bohol expansion

**Breakeven Analysis:**
- Fixed costs (team salary): PHP 600K/year (2027)
- Contribution margin per customer: PHP 2.5K × 80% = PHP 2K/month × 12 = PHP 24K LTV
- Breakeven: 600K / 24K = 25 customers (reached by Q2 2027)
- **Implication:** Only need 25 customers to cover costs; upside is significant
