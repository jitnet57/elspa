## 🎯 How to Use This Package

### Timeline: Before Series A Close

**Month 1: Preparation (Now)**
1. Read all 5 documents end-to-end
2. Customize numbers/examples to your current metrics
3. Gather missing documents for data room
4. Brief team on talking points (pitch deck + FAQ)
5. Hire investor relations advisor (optional but helpful)

**Month 2: Outreach (June 2026)**
1. Identify target VCs/angels (10-20 investors)
2. Warm introductions from board/advisors
3. Send pitch deck PDF + investor intro email
4. Schedule investor calls (pitch meetings)
5. Track interest & feedback in spreadsheet

**Month 3: Due Diligence (July 2026)**
1. Set up VDR with documents from data room checklist
2. Provide access to serious leads (after LOI)
3. Answer investor questions (use FAQ as guide)
4. Schedule follow-up meetings
5. Negotiate term sheet with lead investor

**Month 4: Close (August 2026)**
1. Finalize legal documents (lawyer-negotiated)
2. Board approval & shareholder signatures
3. Funds wired to company account
4. Cap table updated, preferred stock issued
5. Announce to employees, customers, advisors

---

### Timeline: After Series A Close

**Month 1: Investor Onboarding (September 2026)**
1. Provide board access (email, Slack, tools)
2. Share cap table & financial dashboard
3. Schedule first board meeting
4. Introduce investor to team
5. Outline board communication cadence

**Monthly: Investor Updates (Starting October 2026)**
1. Use investor update template
2. Track metrics in spreadsheet
3. Send on 1st business day of next month
4. Include headline metrics in subject line
5. Archive in shared folder

**Quarterly: Board Meetings (October, January, April)**
1. Prepare detailed board package (5-10 pages)
2. Send materials 1 week before meeting
3. Host 2-hour board meeting
4. Document decisions & action items
5. Send follow-up email with notes

**Annually: Investor Letter (December)**
1. Write comprehensive annual letter
2. Reflect on year, lessons learned
3. Preview next year strategy
4. Share with all investors
5. Archive for reference

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Problem: Investors aren't responding to outreach**
- Solution: Get warm introductions (not cold emails)
- Action: Ask board/advisors for investor intros

**Problem: Investors want different business model**
- Solution: Listen to feedback, don't be defensive
- Action: Update pitch if feedback is consistent across 3+ investors

**Problem: Valuation pushback ("Too high!")**
- Solution: Stand firm on $7.5M pre if metrics support it
- Action: Show comparable companies at similar revenue/growth

**Problem: Investors want larger check ($5M vs. $2.5M)**
- Solution: Only take capital you need
- Action: Explain profitability path with smaller raise

**Problem: Legal negotiations stalling**
- Solution: Hire experienced venture lawyer
- Action: Get samples of "founder-friendly" term sheets
