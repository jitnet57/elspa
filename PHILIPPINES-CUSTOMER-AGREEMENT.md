## 1. DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this Agreement:

1. **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person; includes but not limited to name, address, tax ID (TIN), SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG numbers, salary, bank account details, and identification documents of employees.

2. **"Payroll Data"** means employee compensation, deductions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, income tax, loans), benefits, attendance, and leave records.

3. **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by Client, including but not limited to Payroll Data, business operations, financial data, and employee records.

4. **"Service"** means the payroll processing, tax reporting, contribution administration, and HR compliance services described in Schedule A (Statement of Work).

5. **"Business Day"** means Monday through Friday, excluding Philippine national holidays and declared holidays.

6. **"DPA"** means Data Processing Agreement, as defined in Section 7 (Data Privacy & Security).

7. **"NPC"** means National Privacy Commission (Philippine data protection authority).

8. **"BIR"** means Bureau of Internal Revenue (Philippine tax authority).

9. **"Mandatory Deductions"** means SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, income tax withholding, and other legally required deductions from employee pay.

## 2. SERVICES DESCRIPTION

### 2.1 Scope of Services

Service Provider shall provide the following services to Client:

#### A. **Payroll Processing Services**
- Receiving monthly employee data (names, hours worked, allowances, deductions)
- Calculating gross-to-net compensation
- Processing Mandatory Deductions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, income tax withholding)
- Preparing payroll summaries and pay slips
- Uploading payroll data to Client's HR system (if integrated)
- Providing employee-accessible portal for pay slip viewing

#### B. **Tax Compliance & Reporting**
- Integrating latest BIR income tax tables (updated annually)
- Calculating monthly withholding tax per BIR regulations
- Generating and filing **BIR Form 1601-C** (monthly withholding certificate)
- Preparing **BIR Form 1604-CF** (quarterly reconciliation)
- Assisting with **BIR Form 1700** (annual income tax return) preparation
- E-filing of forms via BIRev system (where permitted)
- Maintaining 5-year audit trail for BIR examination

#### C. **Contribution Administration**
- **SSS:** Calculating monthly contributions (12% per employee), filing **SS Form R-3**, and quarterly reconciliation
- **PhilHealth:** Calculating monthly premiums per employee salary tier, submitting via PhilHealth Online System (PHOS)
- **Pag-IBIG:** Calculating monthly contributions (2% per employee), submitting via Pag-IBIG system
- Tracking contribution receipts and reconciliation
- Providing quarterly contribution statements to Client

#### D. **DOLE & Labor Code Compliance**
- Tracking working hours and overtime (if applicable)
- Leave accrual and leave-taken reporting (Service Incentive Leave, vacation, sick leave, maternity/paternity)
- Separation benefit calculations (if redundancy/closure)
- Workplace safety & compliance documentation (in consultation with Client)
- Annual leave balance reconciliation

#### E. **Employee Records & Reporting**
- Maintaining confidential employee database (encrypted)
- Generating monthly payroll reports (gross, deductions, net)
- Quarterly and annual payroll summaries
- Year-end 1601-E reporting (if required)
- Employee attestation letter generation (for loan/credit applications)

#### F. **Consultation & Support**
- Monthly email updates on regulatory changes (tax tables, contribution rates, labor law updates)
- Quarterly compliance check-in meetings (via video call)
- On-demand consultation on payroll-related questions (5 business days response time)
- Escalation contact for tax/labor issues

**Services excluded:**
- Legal representation (Client remains responsible for compliance decisions)
- Hiring/termination processing (Client's HR responsibility)
- Workplace disciplinary procedures
- Negotiations with government agencies (Client-led, Service Provider assists)

A detailed Statement of Work (SOW) specifying deliverables, timelines, and client responsibilities shall be attached as **Schedule A** and incorporated into this Agreement.

**Key SOW items to define:**

## 3. FEES & PAYMENT TERMS

### 3.1 Service Fees

Client shall pay Service Provider fees as specified in **Schedule B (Pricing)**.

**Standard pricing model:**
- **Base fee:** Per number of employees processed monthly
- **Minimum fee:** Applicable if fewer employees than minimum threshold
- **Add-on services:** Special reports, integrations, training (separate quote)

**Example fee structure:**
```
1–10 employees:       ₱2,500/month
11–50 employees:      ₱1,800/month per employee
51–100 employees:     ₱1,500/month per employee
101+ employees:       ₱1,200/month per employee + negotiated rate

Plus: One-time setup fee ₱5,000 (initial onboarding, system configuration)
```

### 3.2 Payment Terms

1. **Invoice timing:** Service Provider shall invoice Client within 5 business days of month-end.
2. **Payment deadline:** Client shall pay within **30 days** of invoice date (net-30 terms).
3. **Payment method:** Bank transfer (Client's choice of bank), check, or online payment (GCash, Grab, Maya).
4. **Late payment:** If payment is late, Service Provider may charge **1.5% per month** interest (18% per annum).
5. **Currency:** All fees in **Philippine Pesos (₱)**.

### 3.3 Price Changes

- Annual fee reviews permitted (effective January 1 or agreed date)
- Notice of 30 days required before implementation
- Price changes limited to **maximum 10% increase per year** (unless regulatory/cost-of-living adjustments justify higher)

### 3.4 Taxes & Additional Costs

- Fees do NOT include Value Added Tax (VAT), if applicable
- Service Provider shall issue **VAT invoice** and claim input VAT per BIR regulations
- Client responsible for any increase in costs due to regulatory changes (e.g., new SSS contribution rates)

## 7. DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY (Data Privacy Act 2012)

### 7.1 Data Privacy Act Compliance

Service Provider acknowledges that it processes **Personal Data** (as defined in RA 10173, Data Privacy Act of 2012) on behalf of Client. Both parties shall comply with the Act.

### 7.2 Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Service Provider and Client shall execute a separate **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** that specifies:
- Nature and scope of data processing
- Categories of Personal Data processed
- Duration of processing
- Lawful basis for processing (consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation)
- Technical and organizational security measures
- Data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, portability)
- Sub-processor arrangements (if any)
- Incident response and breach notification procedures

**DPA is attached as Schedule C.**

### 7.3 Employee Consent

Client warrants that:
- All Personal Data provided to Service Provider has been lawfully obtained
- Client has obtained employee **written consent** for data processing (per DPA 2012 requirements)
- Employees have been informed of data processing purposes, recipients, and rights

**Service Provider shall provide Client with a template Consent Form** (included in DPA, Schedule C).

### 7.4 Data Security Measures

Service Provider shall implement:

#### A. **Technical Safeguards**
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 encryption for all stored Personal Data
- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3+ for data transmission (HTTPS)
- **Access control:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for staff access
- **Firewalls & intrusion detection:** Advanced threat protection
- **Regular backups:** Daily backups with encryption (3-month retention minimum)
- **Patch management:** Monthly security updates
- **Vulnerability scanning:** Quarterly penetration testing

#### B. **Organizational Safeguards**
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** Designated individual responsible for DPA compliance
- **Staff training:** Annual Data Privacy Act training for all staff (with sign-off)
- **Access logs:** All access to Personal Data logged (timestamp, user ID, action)
- **Data minimization:** Collect only data necessary for payroll processing
- **Retention policy:** Delete Personal Data 5 years after engagement ends (per BIR requirements)

#### C. **Vendor Management**
- **Sub-processor agreements:** All service providers (cloud, payment, etc.) execute Data Processing Agreements
- **Regular audits:** Annual security audits of sub-processors
- **Incident notification:** Sub-processors required to notify Service Provider of breaches within 24 hours

### 7.5 Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs:

1. **Service Provider shall notify Client** within **24 hours** of discovery
2. **Client and Service Provider shall jointly notify** affected employees within **30 days** (NPC requirement)
3. **NPC notification:** Service Provider and Client shall file incident report with National Privacy Commission within **30 days** of discovery
4. **Investigation:** Service Provider shall conduct investigation and document findings
5. **Remediation:** Service Provider shall implement corrective measures to prevent recurrence

**Notification shall include:**
- Nature of breach (what data, how many individuals, how many records)
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed
- Contact person for inquiries
- NPC complaint process information

### 7.6 Data Subject Rights

**Employees (as data subjects) have the right to:**
- **Access:** Obtain copy of their Personal Data (Client shall facilitate via Service Provider)
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion ("right to be forgotten") where legally permissible
- **Portability:** Receive data in structured format for transfer to another provider

**Client shall process employee requests and forward to Service Provider. Service Provider shall comply within 15 days.**

### 7.7 Data Retention & Deletion

Service Provider shall:
- **Retain data** for **5 years** after service termination (for BIR tax compliance)
- **Delete data** securely after 5-year retention period (shred, demagnetize, or cryptographic deletion)
- **Provide deletion certificate** to Client confirming data destruction
- **Exception:** Data required by law (BIR, NPC order) shall be retained as legally mandated

## 9. TERM & TERMINATION

### 9.1 Initial Term

This Agreement commences on the **Effective Date** and continues for an initial term of **[1 year / 2 years]** (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated as provided herein.

### 9.2 Renewal

Upon expiration of the Initial Term:
- This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive **one-year periods** unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal **30 days before** term expiration.

### 9.3 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason with **60 days written notice** and payment of fees through the notice period.

**Notice procedure:**
- Send formal written notice via email and registered mail to the other party's designated contact
- Include termination date (at least 60 days from notice date)
- Termination is effective on the date specified in notice

### 9.4 Termination for Cause

Service Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately if:
- Client fails to pay invoices for **more than 30 days** after due date (despite written demand)
- Client materially breaches its data security or confidentiality obligations
- Client misuses Personal Data or violates DPA requirements
- Client engages in illegal activity that affects payroll/tax compliance

**Client may terminate this Agreement immediately if:**
- Service Provider materially breaches SLA (Section 5.1) and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice
- Service Provider suffers a data breach and fails to remediate within 10 days
- Service Provider loses required registrations (BIR, SEC, etc.)
- Service Provider is acquired by a competitor (Client may request alternative service provider)

**Procedure for cause termination:**
1. Send written notice of breach
2. Allow 10–15 days to cure (except imminent harm situations)
3. If uncured, send termination notice with effective date

### 9.5 Obligations Upon Termination

Upon termination of this Agreement:

**Service Provider shall:**
- Complete all pending payroll processing through termination date
- Deliver all Client data and work product within 10 business days
- Provide transition assistance (5 hours at no charge; additional hours at agreed rate)
- Delete all Client Personal Data (except legally required retention) within 30 days
- Provide deletion certificate and audit trail documentation

**Client shall:**
- Pay all outstanding invoices within 30 days of termination date
- Retrieve all data and files promptly
- Redirect government correspondence to new service provider or internal team
- Ensure continuity of payroll and tax compliance

### 9.6 Survival

The following provisions survive termination:
- Section 6 (Confidentiality)—for 5 years
- Section 7 (Data Privacy)—for 5 years (consistent with record retention)
- Section 10 (Indemnification)
- Section 12 (Limitation of Liability)
- Section 13 (Dispute Resolution)

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 13.1 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Republic of the Philippines**, without regard to conflicts of law principles.

### 13.2 Jurisdiction & Venue

Both parties irrevocably submit to the **exclusive jurisdiction** and **exclusive venue** of the courts in:
- **For Cebu-based Client:** Regional Trial Court of Cebu City
- **For Bohol-based Client:** Regional Trial Court of Tagbilaran City
- **For other regions:** Court of appropriate jurisdiction in Client's province

### 13.3 Dispute Resolution Process

**Before initiating litigation:**

1. **Informal Resolution (14 days):**
   - Either party may provide written notice of dispute
   - Within 5 business days, parties shall meet (in person or via video) to attempt resolution
   - Senior representatives of each party shall participate

2. **Mediation (30 days):**
   - If unresolved, parties shall submit to non-binding mediation
   - Mediator selected by mutual agreement
   - Mediation conducted in English or Filipino (as agreed)
   - Costs split equally between parties
   - Mediation statements remain confidential (not admissible in court)

3. **Litigation (if mediation fails):**
   - Either party may pursue legal action in appropriate Philippine courts
   - Prevailing party may recover reasonable attorney's fees and court costs

### 13.4 Attorneys' Fees

In any legal action, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and court costs from the non-prevailing party (per Philippine law).
