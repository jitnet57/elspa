## Document 2: THAILAND-PAYROLL-SYSTEM.md (25 KB)

**Purpose**: Thai labor law requirements, compliance deadlines, and ElSpa product specification for payroll automation

**Key Compliance Requirements**:

| Item | Details | Deadline | Penalty if Late |
|------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| **Social Insurance (SI)** | 5% emp + 5% emp (total 10%) | By 15th next month | 1.5% monthly fine |
| **Income Tax Withholding** | Progressive 5-35% brackets | By 15th next month | 1.5% monthly fine |
| **Unemployment Insurance (UI)** | 0.5% emp + 0.5% emp | By 15th next month | 1.5% monthly fine |
| **Annual Tax Report (PND 1)** | Employer withholding summary | May 31 | ฿500-5,000 fine |
| **Employee Certificates (PND 3)** | Issue to all employees | May 31 | Can't file personal tax |
| **SSO Annual Report (Por.Por.14)** | Contribution summary | Jan 31 | ฿500-5K fine |
| **Minimum Wage Check** | Verify pay ≥ regional minimum | Continuous | ฿5K-20K fine per employee |
| **Leave Accrual** | 6 days/year (0.5/month) | Track monthly | ฿5K-100K fine if violated |

**Critical Features for ElSpa Payroll**:
1. ✅ Social Insurance calculator (SSO integration-ready)
2. ✅ Income tax withholding (PND 1/3 generation)
3. ✅ Leave accrual automation (Thai leave rules)
4. ✅ Holiday calendar (13 Thai holidays + Buddhist calendar)
5. ✅ Compliance dashboard (deadline reminders)
6. ✅ Multi-region minimum wage lookup
7. ✅ Therapist commission tracking
8. ✅ Seasonality staff management
9. ✅ Thai language UI + forms
10. ✅ Local payment integration (LINE Pay, Promptay)

**Use This For**:
- Understanding Thai payroll complexity
- Defining payroll MVP features
- Planning legal compliance review
- Setting up accounting partnerships (accountants validate our math)
- Training customer support on Thai labor law

## Document 3: THAILAND-COMPETITORS-ANALYSIS.md (26 KB)

**Purpose**: Competitive landscape, competitor weaknesses, and ElSpa differentiation strategy

**Competitive Landscape**:

```
Tier 1: Global Premium (Mindbody)
├─ Features: Excellent
├─ Price: ฿16.5K-50K/month (too expensive for Thai market)
├─ Localization: None (English only)
├─ Payroll: No Thai payroll (critical gap)
├─ Threat: Low-to-Medium (premium segment only)
└─ Action: Position as "Mindbody for Thailand" at 1/10th cost

Tier 2: Local Weak Players (Zen, Zimple)
├─ Zen Booking (2% adoption)
│  ├─ Strengths: Thai-native, affordable (฿800-1,200)
│  ├─ Weaknesses: NO PAYROLL, weak analytics, outdated tech
│  ├─ Threat: MEDIUM if adds payroll (12-18 months)
│  └─ Action: Beat them on feature + payroll combo
├─ Zimple (<1% adoption)
│  ├─ Strengths: Cheapest (฿600)
│  ├─ Weaknesses: Micro-market, no payroll, poor support
│  ├─ Threat: LOW (will likely fail or be acquired)
│  └─ Action: Ignore; focus on Zen

Tier 3: Free/Indirect (Google, WhatsApp, Accountants)
├─ Google My Business: 40% drive bookings (free, no payroll)
├─ WhatsApp: 95% use for messaging (free, no payroll)
├─ Spreadsheets: 85% primary tool (free, error-prone)
├─ Accountants: 90% use for payroll (฿5K-10K/month, slow)
└─ Action: Position as "all-in-one" vs. patchwork of tools
```

**ElSpa Competitive Advantages**:

| Advantage | Impact | Competitor Gap |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Thai-Native Payroll** | Save ฿60K-120K/year vs accountant | Zen (0), Mindbody (0), Accountants (slow) |
| **Affordable Pricing** | ฿1K-2K/month vs ฿16.5K Mindbody | 10x cheaper than Mindbody |
| **Therapist Features** | Drive adoption + retention | All competitors miss |
| **Tourist Features** | Capture 40% of Bangkok/Phuket revenue | Zen (weak), Mindbody (generic) |
| **Seasonality Tools** | Maximize Nov-Feb surge, minimize May-Aug trough | No competitor offers |
| **Payment Gateway Suite** | LINE Pay, Promptay, TrueeMoney all integrated | Zen (limited), Mindbody (extra fees) |
| **Local Support** | Thai language, Thai team, Thai timezone | Mindbody (US only), Accountants (local but slow) |

**Threat Timeline**:
- **Months 0-6** (Now): Low threat; build fast before Zen responds
- **Months 6-12**: Medium threat; Zen may start payroll features
- **Months 12-18**: CRITICAL; if Zen adds payroll + Mindbody enters, market gets competitive
- **Months 18-24**: Window closes; must be clear market leader by then

**Use This For**:
- Validating value proposition
- Planning competitive messaging
- Identifying go-to-market priorities
- Setting speed benchmarks (ship payroll by Nov 2025)
- Sales team talking points
