## 📋 QUICK START

**You are a C-level executive at ElSpa.**

This system enables **repeatable, data-driven** weekly operations for:
- **CEO** — Strategic direction, revenue targets, risk management
- **CFO** — Financial health, cash runway, profitability tracking
- **CTO** — Technology stability, product velocity, team health

Each executive has a **weekly rhythm** (specific daily times + artifacts) and **monthly reviews** to ensure alignment and accountability.

## 🚨 RED ALERT THRESHOLDS

**If any of these trigger, escalate immediately to CEO:**

```
Financial Red Alerts:
├─ Cash runway < 9 months
├─ Burn rate > 110% of budget
├─ Revenue < 80% of daily target
└─ Churn rate > 3% monthly

Operational Red Alerts:
├─ System uptime < 99% (SLA breach)
├─ Error rate > 1%
├─ Critical customer issue unresolved >2 hours
└─ Any payment processing failure

Team Red Alerts:
├─ Key person resignation
├─ Sprint velocity drops >30%
├─ Customer NPS declining trend
└─ Competitor alert (major news)
```

## 🤝 ACCOUNTABILITY

**Each executive commits to:**

1. **Attend all scheduled meetings** (no exceptions without advance notice)
2. **Submit metrics on time** (weekly KPIs by Monday 09:00)
3. **Complete assigned action items** (by stated deadline)
4. **Escalate blockers immediately** (don't wait for weekly meeting)
5. **Communicate decisions to team** (within 24 hours of decision)
6. **Track outcomes** (review actual vs forecast at 30/60/90 days)

**CEO is accountable for:**
- Weekly business review quality
- Decision timeliness (go/no-go by Friday)
- Board communication (weekly update)
- Team alignment (all exec decisions clearly communicated)

**CFO is accountable for:**
- Cash position (>12 months runway maintained)
- Forecast accuracy (variance <5% from plan)
- Budget compliance (all departments <110% of budget)
- Monthly reporting (on time, accurate)

**CTO is accountable for:**
- Uptime SLA (99.9% maintained)
- Sprint delivery (velocity consistent ±10%)
- Security/compliance (zero critical vulnerabilities)
- Technical quality (low production defects)
