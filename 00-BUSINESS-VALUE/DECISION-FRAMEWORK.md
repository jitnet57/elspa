# DECISION FRAMEWORK & GO/NO-GO GATES
**ElSpa Leadership System** | Last Updated: 2026-05-29

---

## 📋 OVERVIEW
This document provides decision-making frameworks for ElSpa's C-level executives to make strategic go/no-go decisions on expansion, hiring, feature prioritization, and pivots.

**Core Principle:** Every major decision should be data-driven, have clear success criteria, and include mitigation for downside risk.

---

## 🎯 EXPANSION DECISION FRAMEWORK
**Owner:** CEO (with CFO, CTO, VP Ops input)  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly (as new markets emerge)  
**Timeline:** 4-week evaluation → Decision → 8-week execution

## 💼 FEATURE PRIORITIZATION DECISION FRAMEWORK
**Owner:** CEO + VP Product  
**Review Cadence:** Weekly (Friday decision day)  
**Timeline:** 1-2 hour decision meeting

### Scoring Methodology

**Each feature scored on 5-point scale (1=Low, 5=High):**

```
FEATURE SCORING MATRIX:

Feature: [Name]

CUSTOMER VALUE (1-5):
├─ How badly do customers want this? (1=nice-to-have, 5=critical)
├─ Interview evidence: [Quote from customer]
├─ Adoption estimate: X% of customer base
├─ Revenue impact if built: $XX,XXX/month
└─ Score: X/5

BUSINESS IMPACT (1-5):
├─ Does this enable revenue growth? (1=no, 5=core)
├─ Competitive advantage? (1=none, 5=defensible moat)
├─ Market opportunity? (1=niche, 5=mass market)
├─ Strategic fit with roadmap? (1=off-track, 5=critical)
└─ Score: X/5

DEVELOPMENT COMPLEXITY (1-5):
├─ Technical difficulty? (1=trivial, 5=very complex)
├─ Dependencies on other teams? (1=none, 5=many)
├─ Testing effort required? (1=simple, 5=extensive)
├─ Risk of breaking other features? (1=low, 5=high)
└─ INVERSE Score: X/5 (flip this - lower is better)

RISK LEVEL (1-5):
├─ Execution risk? (1=low, 5=high)
├─ Market risk (will customers buy)? (1=low, 5=high)
├─ Technical risk (will it work)? (1=low, 5=high)
├─ Compliance/legal risk? (1=low, 5=high)
└─ INVERSE Score: X/5 (flip this - lower is better)

TOTAL PRIORITY SCORE:
(Customer Value × 2) + Business Impact + (Complexity × -1) + (Risk × -1) = X

Weighted Calculation Example:
(5 × 2) + 4 + (-2) + (-1) = 10 + 4 - 2 - 1 = 11 points
```

## 🔄 PIVOT/CONTINUE DECISION FRAMEWORK
**Owner:** CEO (with full leadership team input)  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly (1st Monday)  
**Timeline:** Full-day strategic review

### Pivot Triggers

**Consider a pivot if:**

```
QUANTITATIVE TRIGGERS:
├─ MRR growth rate < X% for 2+ consecutive months
├─ Customer churn rate > X%
├─ NPS score < X (indicating market rejection)
├─ CAC > LTV / 3 (unit economics broken)
├─ Cash runway < X months (burning faster than planned)
└─ Team attrition >X% (culture/execution issues)

QUALITATIVE TRIGGERS:
├─ Market landscape changed (new competitor, regulatory change)
├─ Key customer feedback: "We need [different feature]"
├─ Founding team losing confidence in direction
├─ Top talent leaving (reason: "Wrong strategy")
├─ Product-market fit not achieved after X months
└─ Customers buying for different reason than we built
```

## 📚 DECISION-MAKING BEST PRACTICES

### Before Making a Decision:

1. **Gather data** (1-3 hours)
   - Customer interviews or surveys
   - Financial modeling
   - Competitive research
   - Team input & concerns

2. **Frame options** (30 min)
   - What are we actually deciding?
   - What are the X alternatives?
   - What's the cost of not deciding?

3. **Apply framework** (30 min)
   - Score each option against criteria
   - Calculate financial impact
   - Identify key risks

4. **Get buy-in** (before decision day)
   - CFO confident in financials?
   - VP Operations confident in execution?
   - CEO confident in strategy?

### Making the Decision:

1. **Clear recommendation** (state upfront: GO / NO-GO / DELAY)
2. **Explain the data** (show your work)
3. **Address concerns** (acknowledge objections, explain mitigation)
4. **State success criteria** (how will we know if right decision?)
5. **Assign owner** (who is accountable for execution?)
6. **Set checkpoint** (when will we re-evaluate?)

### After Decision:

1. **Document** (log in decision tracker)
2. **Communicate** (all stakeholders, especially those who disagreed)
3. **Execute** (owner begins work)
4. **Monitor** (weekly status updates vs success criteria)
5. **Evaluate** (post-mortem if outcome differs from forecast)
