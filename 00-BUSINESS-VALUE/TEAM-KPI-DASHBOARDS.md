# TEAM KPI DASHBOARDS
**ElSpa Leadership System** | Last Updated: 2026-05-29

---

## 📋 OVERVIEW
This document defines department-level KPIs for Sales, Product, Customer Success, and Finance teams. Each dashboard refreshes weekly and drives team accountability.

**Key Principles:**
- Each team owns 3-5 critical metrics
- Weekly tracking (trending, variance analysis)
- Monthly deep-dive reviews
- Monthly targets cascade from company goals

## ✅ KPI OWNERSHIP & ACCOUNTABILITY

**Each department lead is accountable for:**
- [ ] Weekly KPI submission (by Monday 9:00 AM)
- [ ] Variance explanations (if >5% from target)
- [ ] Action items (if any metric red)
- [ ] Monthly deep-dive review
- [ ] Team alignment on KPIs

**CEO Responsibilities:**
- [ ] Review all KPIs weekly
- [ ] 1:1 with each leader to discuss variances
- [ ] Escalate red metrics immediately
- [ ] Celebrate green metrics / progress
- [ ] Board reporting (monthly)
