### 🛡️ Document 3: GLOBAL-RISK-MANAGEMENT.md (40KB, 1,069 lines)

**Core Content:**
- **Top 20 identified risks** categorized by severity (5 critical, 10 high, 15 medium)

**Critical Risks (5):**
1. **Regulatory compliance changes (Korea)** — Payroll tax law updates quarterly
   - Mitigation: Dedicated compliance officer, quarterly updates, customer buffer
   - Go/No-Go Gate: Monthly monitoring, Q1 decision point

2. **Competitive response from global players** — Zenoti/Mindbody localizing to Korea/PH
   - Timeline: Expected Months 12-18
   - Mitigation: Lock-in customers (3-year contracts), geographic diversification
   - Go/No-Go Gate: Q2 (Month 6) assessment

3. **Customer churn acceleration** — If >5% monthly, business dies
   - Threshold: 3-4% monthly is break-even rate
   - Mitigation: Proactive support, onboarding excellence, NPS >50
   - Go/No-Go Gate: Monthly tracking, RED alert if >4.5%

4. **Currency collapse (severe devaluation)** — KRW/PHP 20-30% devaluation
   - Risk by region: Low (Korea) to Medium (Philippines)
   - Mitigation: Natural hedging (costs/revenue in same currency)
   - Go/No-Go Gate: Quarterly currency monitoring

5. **Platform security breach / data loss** — Customer data exposure
   - Impact: Legal liability, customer churn 50%+, operational shutdown
   - Mitigation: SOC 2 Type II, encryption, incident response plan
   - Go/No-Go Gate: Monthly security checklist

**High Risks (10):**
- Market adoption slower than forecast
- Localization complexity underestimated
- Team retention / key person risk
- Payment processing failures
- Regulatory fines or shutdowns
- 5 more operational risks
