## Weekly Operations Review

### 1.1 Schedule & Participants

**Time:** Every Monday, 9:00 AM UTC  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Location:** Conference Room A + Zoom (zoom://elspa-ops-weekly)  
**Attendees (Required):**
- CEO / President
- COO / Operations Director
- VP Engineering
- VP Product
- VP Sales
- VP Finance
- Regional Directors (all 5 countries)

**Attendees (Optional):**
- Board members (invited)
- Marketing lead (as needed)
- Customer success lead (as needed)

   ✓ Upcoming closes (next 30 days)
   ✓ Regional sales performance

5. PRODUCT UPDATES (10 min) — VP Product
   ✓ Completed features (this week)
   ✓ In-progress roadmap items
   ✓ Customer feedback themes
   ✓ Feature requests vs. bugs ratio

   ✓ Weekly revenue vs. forecast
   ✓ Burn rate vs. budget
   ✓ Upcoming payroll/expenses
   ✓ Cash position & runway

7. REGIONAL PERFORMANCE (20 min) — Regional Directors
   Korea:
     • Regional MRR, customer count
     • Top issues or opportunities
     • Staffing/capacity status

     [Same structure, 3 min each]

   ✓ Key decisions needed
   ✓ Escalated issues
   ✓ Risks requiring action
   ✓ Next week priorities

   ✓ Review action items from last week
   ✓ Assign new action items
   ✓ Confirm next week's agenda

### 4.2 Decision-Making Process

**4-Step Process for Major Decisions:**

```
DECISION-MAKING PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: FRAME THE DECISION (Responsible person)
  • Define the decision clearly
  • Set deadline for decision
  • Identify decision owner (Accountable)
  
  Example:
  Decision: Should we exit Indonesia market?
  Owner: CEO + CFO
  Deadline: June 15, 2026
  Context: Current churn 3.8%, LTV:CAC 2.0:1

Step 2: GATHER DATA & OPTIONS (Responsible person + Consulted)
  • 3-5 options with trade-offs
  • Financial impact analysis
  • Risk assessment
  • Stakeholder input
  
  Options for Indonesia:
  A) Exit market (save $50K/mo burn)
    - Pro: Reduce burn, focus resources
    - Con: Sunk costs ($30K), 105 customers lost
    
  B) Continue with cost reduction (optimize unit economics)
    - Pro: Potential turnaround, growing market
    - Con: Uncertain, requires investment
    
  C) Sell to local partner (if buyer found)
    - Pro: Get working capital back
    - Con: Loss of control, uncertain timeline

Step 3: RECOMMEND & DECIDE (Accountable person)
  • Present recommendation to decision maker
  • Approval (or request more info)
  • Clear decision communicated
  
  Recommendation: OPTION B (Continue with cost reduction)
  Rationale: Indonesia market is growing, churn can be addressed with pricing & product changes. Potential long-term upside justifies continued investment for 6 more months.
  
  Approval: CEO + Board sign off

Step 4: COMMUNICATE & IMPLEMENT (All stakeholders informed)
  • Decision communicated to affected teams
  • Timeline for implementation
  • Success metrics tracked
  • Quarterly review for adjustment
  
  Communication:
  - Regional Director Indonesia: Approved for 6-month turnaround
  - Product: Prioritize Indonesia UX improvements
  - Finance: Budget for cost optimization analysis
  - Metrics: Track LTV:CAC improvement, churn reduction
```

### 4.3 Escalation Protocol

**When decisions can't be made locally:**

```
ESCALATION LADDER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Level 1: Team Lead → Department VP
  Threshold: Decision impacts budget >$5K or timeline >2 weeks
  Timeline: 24 hours
  
Level 2: Department VP → CEO
  Threshold: Decision impacts strategy or revenue >$10K
  Timeline: 48 hours
  
Level 3: CEO → Board
  Threshold: Decision impacts capital raise, M&A, major pivot
  Timeline: Board meeting (or emergency session)
  
Example: Product wants to deprecate core feature (risky)
  Level 1: CPO → CEO (is this strategically sound?)
  Level 2: CEO → Board (could affect customer retention)
  Result: Board decision required (affects company value)
```

### 5.3 KPI Definitions & Formulas

**Key Metrics Glossary:**

```
FINANCIAL METRICS:

MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
  = Sum of all active subscription charges for current month
  = Σ(subscription_charge) where status='active'
  Importance: Top-line health metric

ARR (Annual Run Rate)
  = MRR × 12
  Importance: Annual projection for planning

Gross Margin
  = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100%
  = (Revenue - Therapist_Payments - Payment_Processing) / Revenue
  Target: 60%+ (healthy SaaS)

Burn Rate (Monthly)
  = Revenue - Operating_Expenses
  Current: -$8,200 (losing $8.2K/month)
  Runway = Cash / Burn_Rate (months)

Rule of 40
  = YoY_Growth_Rate (%) + EBITDA_Margin (%)
  Healthy target: >40
  Current: 28% + (-18%) = 10% (needs improvement)

───────────────────────────────────────────────

CUSTOMER METRICS:

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
  = Total_Sales_Marketing_Cost / New_Customers_Acquired
  Current: $114 (blended)
  Used for: Cost efficiency assessment

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
  = ARPU × (1 / Monthly_Churn_Rate) × Gross_Margin
  = Average_Revenue_Per_User / Monthly_Churn_Rate
  Current: $892 (global average)

LTV:CAC Ratio
  = LTV / CAC
  Healthy: >3:1 (every $1 spent returns $3+)
  Current: 4.2:1 (excellent)

CAC Payback Period
  = CAC / (ARPU × Gross_Margin) × 30 days
  Current: 4.2 months (healthy, target <4 months)

Churn Rate (Monthly)
  = Churned_Customers / Start_Month_Customers × 100%
  Current: 2.3% (target <2.0%)

Net Dollar Retention (NDR)
  = (MRR_Previous + Expansion - Churn) / MRR_Previous × 100%
  Healthy: 120%+ (expansion outpaces churn)

───────────────────────────────────────────────

OPERATIONAL METRICS:

System Uptime
  = (Total_Time - Downtime) / Total_Time × 100%
  Target: 99.9% (43.2 min/month acceptable downtime)
  Current: 99.82% (failing SLA)

Response Time (P95)
  = 95th percentile of response latencies
  Target: <500ms
  Current: 425ms (healthy)

Error Rate
  = Failed_Requests / Total_Requests × 100%
  Target: <1.5%
  Current: 0.74% (healthy)

Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR)
  = Time from incident start to restoration
  Target: <30 minutes for P1
  Used for: Incident response efficiency
```
