### Q3: Why spas specifically? Why not become a horizontal HR/payroll platform?

**Answer:**
**Vertical focus is our competitive advantage.** Horizontal platforms (Workday, QuickBooks) can't optimize for spa-specific requirements. Here's why spas are defensible:

**Spa-specific pain points (horizontal platforms ignore):**
1. Commission tracking (10-30% per service, by service type)
2. Multi-position pay (therapist + receptionist roles)
3. Cash advance tracking (common in Asia, niche compliance)
4. 13th month bonus (Philippines/Thailand requirement)
5. Public holiday premium (100-200% variation by country)
6. Therapist utilization optimization (core to spa profitability)

**Horizontal platforms struggle:**
- Workday: $500-1000/month, overkill for 15-person spa
- QuickBooks: Built for general business, not spa math
- Guidepoint: European focus, no scheduling integration
- None combine: Payroll + booking + compliance

**Our strategy:**
- **Year 1-2**: Own the spa market ($44M ARR possible)
- **Year 2-3**: Expand to adjacent verticals (hair salons, dental clinics with similar pain points)
- **Exit strategy**: Become acqui-hire target for larger platforms (Mindbody, Acuity) seeking vertical expertise

**Why this works:**
- Easier to land customers (niche trust, word-of-mouth)
- Higher retention (vertical switching costs)
- Better unit economics (ASP $4,800 vs. horizontal $200-300)
- Defensible moat (AI + compliance expertise)
