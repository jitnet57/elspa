## 🚀 Strategic Priorities by Market

### Korea
**Priority**: Direct sales to premium spas + Payment optimization
- High ARPU (₩2M/month)
- Mature market (less growth headroom)
- Focus: Quality over quantity
- Risk: Slow sales cycle (3-4 weeks)

### Philippines
**Priority**: Accountant partnerships + Cebu dominance
- Accountants as 2nd channel (10-15% referrals)
- High growth potential (50% YoY market growth)
- Focus: Rapid scaling in Visayas region
- Risk: GCash wallet limits for some users

### Thailand
**Priority**: Accountant network leverage + Hotel chain pilots
- Best CAC (฿50K via partnerships)
- Accountants = trusted channel
- Hotel chains = high-value customers (฿75K+/month)
- Focus: Become "accountant-recommended" solution
- Risk: Requires relationship building upfront

### Vietnam
**Priority**: Direct sales + Momo integration excellence
- Fastest growing payment ecosystem
- Zalo messaging (preferred channel)
- High LTV:CAC ratio (72x)
- Focus: Build brand early in market
- Risk: Competitive intensity growing

### Indonesia
**Priority**: Jakarta dominance + Bali luxury resort expansion
- Largest market opportunity (275M population)
- Resort spas = premium segment (IDR 50K+/month)
- GoPay/OVO mature ecosystem
- Focus: Luxury positioning in Bali
- Risk: Geographic dispersion (archipelago)

## 🎓 Lessons Learned & Best Practices

### Payment Processing
```
Most to Least Friction:
1. PromptPay (Thailand) - Frictionless, instant
2. GoPay/OVO (Indonesia) - 2-3 minute settlement
3. Momo (Vietnam) - Real-time
4. GCash (Philippines) - 1-2 hour delay common
5. Korean bank transfers - T+1 settlement

Recommendation:
- Offer multiple payment options in each country
- Monitor payment velocity (% of customers per method)
- Build 1-2 day float for settlement timing
```

### Localization Priority
```
Must-Have (Day 1):
- UI language
- Payment integration
- Tax invoice format
- Currency formatting

Nice-to-Have (Month 1):
- Customer support in local language
- Case studies in local language
- Marketing collateral

Future (Month 3+):
- Local payment reconciliation reports
- Regional compliance customization
```

### Partnership Strategy
```
Most Effective: Thailand (Accountant-led)
- CAC: ฿50K (1/5 of direct sales cost)
- Conversion: 60%+ from trusted referrals
- Recurring: Partners refer 2-3 spas/month

Second: Philippines (Accountant + Hotel chains)
- CAC: ₱100K via accountants
- Conversion: 50%+ from partnerships

Direct Sales Strongest: Korea, Vietnam, Indonesia
- CAC: Higher but full control of narrative
- Conversion: 40-50%
- Relationship-based
```
