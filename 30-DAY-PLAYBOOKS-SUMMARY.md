## 🎓 Key Learning Points

### What Works Across All Markets
1. **Payment integration urgency** - Solve by Day 3
2. **Local language UI** - Critical for adoption
3. **Partnership leverage** - 30-40% of revenue potential
4. **Data migration quality** - Biggest onboarding blocker
5. **Staff training rigor** - Determines customer success

### Market-Specific Wins
- **Korea**: Premium positioning, direct relationship building
- **Philippines**: Accountant partnerships, rapid onboarding
- **Thailand**: Low CAC via partnerships, hotel chain pilots
- **Vietnam**: Zalo communication, high LTV:CAC ratio
- **Indonesia**: Resort spa positioning, GoPay/OVO excellence

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
1. Rushing payment gateway without testing
2. Skipping legal registration (causes contract issues)
3. Poor data migration planning (delays onboarding by weeks)
4. Insufficient staff training (leads to support tickets)
5. Unfamiliar tax/compliance rules (causes invoice errors)
