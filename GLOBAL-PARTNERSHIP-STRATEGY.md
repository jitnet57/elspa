## 1. PARTNERSHIP TYPES & VALUE MODELS

### 1.1 Strategic Partnership Taxonomy

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PARTNERSHIP TYPES: ElSpa Manager             │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TYPE 1: CHANNEL PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Partners resell ElSpa to their customer base
├─ Examples: Franchisors, accounting firms, HR consultants
├─ Revenue Model: Commission-based (10-20% of first year)
├─ Effort Level: Medium (partner handles sales, we handle service)
└─ Target: 5-10 per region

TYPE 2: INTEGRATION PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Connect ElSpa with complementary tools
├─ Examples: Payment processors, accounting software, POS systems
├─ Revenue Model: Revenue share (0-5%) + co-marketing
├─ Effort Level: High (requires API/technical work)
└─ Target: 2-3 per region

TYPE 3: CO-MARKETING PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Joint campaigns, webinars, content
├─ Examples: Beauty associations, wellness brands, tourism boards
├─ Revenue Model: No direct commission (lead generation value)
├─ Effort Level: Low (mostly content + promotion)
└─ Target: 10-15 per region

TYPE 4: STRATEGIC RESELLER PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Reseller becomes semi-exclusive channel
├─ Examples: Large accounting firms, HR consultancy groups
├─ Revenue Model: Commission + exclusivity arrangement
├─ Effort Level: Very High (dedicated support + co-GTM)
└─ Target: 1-3 per region

TYPE 5: PLATFORM/ECOSYSTEM PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Become embedded in partner's platform
├─ Examples: Hotel management systems, salon software (add-on)
├─ Revenue Model: Revenue share (5-15% of partner revenue)
├─ Effort Level: Very High (long implementation)
└─ Target: 1 per region (aspirational)

TYPE 6: REFERRAL PARTNERSHIPS
├─ Description: Lightweight partnership for lead sharing
├─ Examples: Spa consultants, business coaches, freelancers
├─ Revenue Model: 10-15% commission (low-touch)
├─ Effort Level: Very Low (self-service)
└─ Target: 20-30 per region
```

### 1.2 Value Model by Partner Type

#### TYPE 1: Channel Partnerships - "Franchisors"

```
VALUE PROPOSITION FOR PARTNER (Franchisor):
├─ Solution to franchisee pain: "Solve operational chaos at scale"
├─ Revenue opportunity: White-label or resell opportunity
├─ Risk reduction: Ensure franchisee success (reduces churn)
├─ Competitive advantage: Differentiate franchise offering
├─ Support: Dedicated support for franchisees

PARTNER BENEFITS:
├─ Commission: 15-20% of first year ACV per franchisee
├─ Co-marketing: Feature franchisee success stories
├─ Training: Free training materials for franchisee onboarding
├─ Reporting: Dedicated partner dashboard + quarterly reports
└─ Escalation: Direct support channel for partner

ELSPA BENEFITS:
├─ Lead generation: Access to franchise network (100-500 potential customers)
├─ Trust: Franchisors' endorsement builds credibility
├─ Revenue: 15-20% cut of every franchisee customer
├─ Network effects: Multi-location mandate = higher LTV
└─ Retention: Franchisees stay due to corporate mandate

EXAMPLE PARTNER: "K-Beauty Franchise Association"
├─ Members: 1,200 spas across Korea
├─ Estimated TAM: $30M (targeting 50 franchisees)
├─ Expected contribution: $180K-250K ARR
└─ Timeline: 6-9 months to first deal
```

## 2. PARTNER IDENTIFICATION & SEGMENTATION

### 2.1 Partner Screening Criteria

```
✅ IDEAL PARTNER CHARACTERISTICS

Reach (Must have)
├─ Customer base: 50+ potential ElSpa customers
├─ Industry alignment: Healthcare, wellness, beauty, hospitality
└─ Geography: Presence in target market (Korea, Philippines, etc.)

Access (Must have)
├─ Decision maker: Accessible to partnership discussion
├─ Reputation: Trusted in their industry
└─ Network: Strong relationships with potential customers

Alignment (Must have)
├─ Customer pain: Solves for their customer's pain
├─ Values: Aligned with ElSpa's mission
└─ Vision: Long-term relationship potential (not one-off)

Capability (Should have)
├─ Resources: Team to manage partnership
├─ Technology: Can integrate (if tech partnership)
├─ Sales ability: Can sell or refer effectively
└─ Support: Can provide customer support (if reseller)

Commercial Viability (Must have)
├─ Deal size: Worth the effort (min $50K annual opportunity)
├─ Margins: Economics work for both parties
├─ Growth: Scalable model (not one-off deals)
└─ Timeline: Can close first deal within 6 months

❌ RED FLAGS (Avoid these partners)
├─ Declining industry/company
├─ Poor reputation or customer complaints
├─ Unreasonable margin expectations (>50%)
├─ Wanting exclusivity without guaranteed volume
├─ Limited ability to support customers
├─ Competing products already in portfolio
└─ Slow decision-making (>3 months to decide)
```

## 4. PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT TEMPLATES

### 4.1 Standard Channel Partnership Agreement

```markdown
# PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT - CHANNEL RESELLER

**EFFECTIVE DATE:** [DATE]  
**PARTIES:** ElSpa Manager ("Company") and [PARTNER NAME] ("Partner")

## 1. GRANT OF RIGHTS
The Company grants Partner a non-exclusive right to refer and resell 
ElSpa Manager services to spa and wellness businesses within 
[TERRITORY] (the "Territory").

## 2. PARTNER RESPONSIBILITIES
- Actively market ElSpa Manager to target customers in Territory
- Provide qualified customer referrals (min. 2-3 per month)
- Assist with customer onboarding and training
- Support customers for level 1 support issues
- Maintain professional brand standards

## 3. COMPANY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Provide marketing materials and sales collateral
- Deliver product implementation and training
- Provide technical support and product updates
- Process referral commissions monthly
- Escalate complex customer issues to dedicated team

## 4. FINANCIAL TERMS

### 4.1 Commission Structure
- **First Year:** 15% of Annual Contract Value (ACV) per referred customer
- **Renewal:** 5% of renewal ACV (years 2+)
- **Upgrade:** 15% of incremental ACV (if customer expands)
- **Minimum Threshold:** 2-3 qualified referrals per quarter (no minimum deal size)

### 4.2 Payment Terms
- Commissions paid monthly (within 30 days of customer payment)
- Quarterly business reviews (QBR) with commission summary
- Partner dashboard with real-time commission tracking

### 4.3 Performance Incentives
- **Tier 1:** 3-5 referrals/quarter → Base commission
- **Tier 2:** 6-10 referrals/quarter → 17% commission
- **Tier 3:** 10+ referrals/quarter → 20% commission + marketing support

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION
- **Initial Term:** 12 months
- **Renewal:** Auto-renews annually unless either party provides 60 days' notice
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice after Year 1
- **Effect of Termination:** 
  - Partner loses commission rights on new referrals
  - Partner retains commission rights on customers referred during contract

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
- Both parties maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Partner may not white-label or rebrand ElSpa without written consent
- Partner may not modify ElSpa product or use trademark without approval

## 7. LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
- Each party is responsible for its own representations
- Partner indemnifies Company from claims related to Partner's actions
- Limitation of liability: [Standard cap]

## 8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of [JURISDICTION].

---
**SIGNATURE PAGE:**

Company Representative:  
[Name/Title] _____________________________ Date: __________

Partner Representative:  
[Name/Title] _____________________________ Date: __________
```
