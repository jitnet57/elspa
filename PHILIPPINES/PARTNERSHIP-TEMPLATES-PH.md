---

### 1. SERVICES

**1.1 Track A (B2B API)**:
ElSpa shall provide access to its Payroll Calculation API (Philippine-compliant) including:
- REST API endpoints for payroll calculation
- SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG integration
- BIR tax withholding calculation
- Night shift premium automation (20%)
- Holiday/Sunday premium calculation (30%)
- 13th month bonus computation
- 99.95% uptime SLA

**1.2 Track B (Healthcare SaaS)**:
ElSpa shall provide access to its Healthcare Caregiver Management Platform including:
- Caregiver scheduling (drag-and-drop)
- Automated payroll calculation
- Skill/certification tracking
- DOH compliance reporting
- Mobile app (SMS + iOS/Android)
- Training and onboarding (included)

---

### 2. PRICING & PAYMENT TERMS

**Track A Pricing Options**:

**Option 1: Per-Transaction Model**
- Price: ₱250 per payroll calculation
- Payment: Monthly invoice (calculated transactions)
- Minimum: None
- Volume discount: ₱200 per transaction (10,000+/month)

**Option 2: Flat Licensing**
- Price: ₱50,000/month (unlimited API calls)
- Payment: Due on 1st of month
- Minimum: 12-month commitment
- Discount: 10% for annual prepayment (₱540K/year)

**Option 3: White-Label**
- Price: ₱75,000/month
- Partner branding: Full white-label
- Support: Included (24/7 email, <4hr response)
- Updates: Automatic (no version control needed)

**Track B Pricing**:
- Price: ₱12,500/month per facility
- Payment: Due on 1st of month
- First month: FREE (trial)
- Minimum: 12-month contract (after trial)

**Payment Method**:
- Bank transfer (BDO/BPI/Metrobank)
- Credit card (Visa/Mastercard, +2.5% fee)
- Online: GCash/PayMaya (if <₱50K)

**Late Payment**: 5% late fee per month after due date

---

### 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**3.1 ElSpa IP Rights**:
Partner acknowledges that ElSpa retains all intellectual property rights in:
- API code and algorithms
- Tax calculation logic
- Payroll formulas
- Mobile app code
- Documentation

**3.2 Partner IP Rights**:
ElSpa shall not claim ownership of:
- Partner's customer data
- Partner's branding/logos
- Partner's integrations (if custom)

**3.3 White-Label Exception (Track A Option 3)**:
If Partner chooses white-label, Partner may:
- Rebrand as own product
- Use own domain/app
- ElSpa provides code (source code NDA required)

---

### 4. DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY

**4.1 Data Protection**:
Both parties shall comply with:
- RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- BIR data handling requirements
- SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG data standards

**4.2 Data Ownership**:
- Customer data (Track B): Partner owns. ElSpa is processor.
- Employee payroll data: Partner owns. ElSpa is processor.
- NPS/feedback data (Track C): Partner owns. ElSpa owns aggregated insights.

**4.3 Encryption**:
- All data in transit: TLS 1.3
- All data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- API auth: JWT tokens (8-hour expiry)

**4.4 Audit Rights**:
Partner may audit ElSpa's security practices:
- Annual SOC 2 Type II audit (provided to Partner)
- Right to audit on 30-day notice
- Right to data export anytime

---

### 5. LIABILITY & WARRANTIES

**5.1 Service Warranty**:
ElSpa warrants:
- 99.95% uptime SLA (monthly average)
- Payroll calculation ≥99.95% accuracy
- Tax compliance with 2026 BIR rules

**5.2 Limitation of Liability**:
ElSpa's total liability is limited to:
- Track A: 3 months of fees paid
- Track B: 1 month of fees paid
- Maximum: ₱500,000 per incident

**5.3 Indemnity**:
ElSpa indemnifies Partner for:
- BIR tax audit errors caused by ElSpa calculation
- SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG underpayment due to ElSpa
- Data breach caused by ElSpa negligence

---

### 6. TERM & TERMINATION

**6.1 Initial Term**:
- Track A: 12 months (auto-renew unless 30-day notice)
- Track B: 12 months (after 30-day free trial)

**6.2 Early Termination**:
- Track A: 30-day notice, no penalty
- Track B: 30-day notice, prorated refund for unused month
- For material breach: Immediate termination

**6.3 Termination Effects**:
Upon termination:
- Partner retains all data (export within 7 days)
- API access disabled immediately
- No refund for past services
- Confidentiality obligations survive

---

### 7. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**7.1 Governing Law**:
This Agreement is governed by Philippine law. Venue: Cebu City Regional Trial Court.

**7.2 Dispute Resolution**:
1. Good faith negotiation (15 days)
2. Mediation via barangay (30 days)
3. Arbitration (CIAC) if unresolved

**7.3 Amendments**:
No amendment valid unless in writing, signed by both parties.

**7.4 Confidentiality**:
Each party shall keep confidential:
- API keys and credentials
- Customer data
- Financial terms
- Proprietary information

- All ElSpa staff

- Discussions about partnership

**Confidential Information Includes**:

- Product roadmap (ElSpa)

**Confidential Information Does NOT Include**:

- Information developed independently

**Obligations**:

- Protect with same care as own confidential information
- Use only for purposes of this partnership

**Duration**: 3 years after agreement termination

**Violations**: Party breaching NDA shall indemnify other party for damages.

## ⚖️ Legal Review Checklist

Before signing any agreement:

**For ElSpa to verify**:
- [ ] Partner is legitimate business (BIR registered)
- [ ] Signatory has authority to bind company
- [ ] No conflicting agreements with competitors

**For Partner to verify**:
- [ ] ElSpa is legitimate PH corporation
- [ ] Insurance (cyber liability, E&O)
- [ ] Data handling practices compliant with RA 10173

**For both parties**:
- [ ] Escalation contacts listed (for disputes)
- [ ] Force majeure clause included (typhoons, pandemics)
- [ ] Insurance requirements specified
