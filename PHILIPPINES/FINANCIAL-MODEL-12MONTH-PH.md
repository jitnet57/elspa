### Revenue Model (3-Track)

#### Track A: Direct B2B (Spa Businesses)
- **Target**: Spa/massage businesses in Cebu/Bohol
- **Price**: ₱4,950/month ($99 equivalent)
- **Contract**: Annual commitment
- **Margin**: 80% (no intermediaries)

#### Track B: Healthcare Partner Channel (Nursing Homes/Hospitals)
- **Target**: Healthcare facilities needing caregiver management
- **Price**: ₱12,500/month ($250 per facility)
- **Contract**: 12-month or monthly
- **Margin**: 60% (partner share 40%)

#### Track C: API Integration (POS/Booking Systems)
- **Target**: Booking system providers, POS software
- **Price**: ₱500/month per location ($10)
- **Contract**: Monthly subscription (usage-based possible)
- **Margin**: 85% (platform split 15%)
