### 4️⃣ Manual Excel + Freelance Services (60% of Market)

#### Current Reality in Cebu/Bohol Spas

```
Typical Setup:
├─ Owner/Manager: Excel file for schedule
├─ Accountant: Separate Excel for payroll
├─ Booking: Google Sheets or paper
├─ Problems:
│  ├─ No real-time sync
│  ├─ Constant manual errors
│  ├─ Tax compliance missed
│  ├─ Staff turnover = knowledge loss
│  └─ Customer complaints about scheduling
│
└─ Cost: ₱500-1,000/month (freelancer) + time waste
```

#### Why Excel Persists
- No upfront cost perception
- Familiarity (everyone knows Excel)
- Fear of "complicated" software
- Previous bad experiences with software

#### Our Advantage
We're 10x cheaper than government systems AND handles spa-specific problems

- **Probability**: 30% (within 12 months)
