## 📍 General Questions

### Q: What is ElSpa exactly?
**A**: ElSpa is a unified automation platform for:
1. **Spas/massage businesses**: Booking + payroll + scheduling
2. **Healthcare facilities**: Caregiver management + payroll
3. **HR platforms**: White-label payroll API

All Philippines-compliant (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR tax).

### Q: Why Philippines-specific? Why not global?
**A**: Because payroll is locally complex. Every country has different rules:
- **USA**: W-2, 1099, state taxes (varies by state)
- **Philippines**: SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR tax + night shift premiums (20%) + 13th month bonus + holiday rates (30%)

Global solutions like Wave can't handle Philippines complexity. We built this specifically for the Filipino market.

### Q: What's the target market?
**A**: Cebu & Bohol region (₱25B spa + healthcare market). Expandable to Manila (₱60B+ nationwide).

### Q: How much does ElSpa cost?
**A**: 
- Spa/massage: ₱4,950/month
- Nursing home: ₱12,500/month
- B2B API partner: ₱50K-100K/month (white-label) or per-transaction ₱250
