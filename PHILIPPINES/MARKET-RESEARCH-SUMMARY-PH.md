## 📉 Market Headwinds & Tailwinds

### Tailwinds (Help ElSpa)
- ✅ Post-pandemic wellness trend
- ✅ Aging population (healthcare demand)
- ✅ Digital transformation (SMEs adopt SaaS)
- ✅ PhilHealth expansion (healthcare spending up)
- ✅ Tax compliance complexity (BIR updates)

### Headwinds (Challenge ElSpa)
- ⚠️ Economic uncertainty (reduced spa spending)
- ⚠️ High price sensitivity (SME affordability)
- ⚠️ Low tech adoption (older business owners)
- ⚠️ Established competitors (Wave, local systems)
- ⚠️ Piracy/unlicensed use (Philippines software culture)

**Net Assessment**: Tailwinds slightly outweigh headwinds (60/40)
