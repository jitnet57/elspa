## 💰 2. Payroll & Tax Compliance

### SSS (Social Security System) - RA 1161, as amended
- ✅ Employer registration required (if 1+ employee)
- ✅ Employee contribution: 11% of monthly salary
- ✅ Employer contribution: 16% of monthly salary
- ✅ Monthly remittance: Due 30 days after end of month
- ✅ ElSpa calculates both automatically

**Withholding from Employee**:
```
If employee earns ₱20,000/month:
├─ Gross: ₱20,000
├─ SSS (11%): ₱2,200 (withheld)
├─ PhilHealth (2.5-3%): ₱500
├─ Pag-IBIG (1-2%): ₱200
├─ Income tax (if applicable): ₱300-500
└─ Net pay: ₱16,800-17,200
```

### PhilHealth (Philippine Health Insurance)
- ✅ Mandatory for all employees earning ₱10,000+/month
- ✅ Contribution rate: 5% (shared between employer & employee)
- ✅ Employer share: 2.5%, Employee share: 2.5%
- ✅ Monthly premium adjustment per law
- ✅ ElSpa auto-calculates current rates

### Pag-IBIG (Home Development Mutual Fund) - RA 9679
- ✅ Mandatory for all employees earning > ₱1,500/month
- ✅ Contribution rate: 1-2% (depends on membership tier)
- ✅ Employer match: Equal to employee contribution
- ✅ ElSpa integrates current rates

### Income Tax Withholding (BIR)
- ✅ Required if monthly taxable income > ₱20,833 (2026 rate)
- ✅ Use Tax Table A (BIR), updated annually
- ✅ Remit monthly withholding to BIR
- ✅ ElSpa database updated with 2026 tax brackets

### 13th Month Bonus - RA 1959
- ✅ Mandatory for all employees with ≥1 month service
- ✅ Amount: 1/12 of annual salary (or ₱20K minimum if higher)
- ✅ Payment due: November 1 - December 15
- ✅ Non-taxable: Only up to ₱30K per employee
- ✅ ElSpa calculates automatically

**Example**:
```
Employee worked Jan-Dec (12 months) at ₱25,000/month:
├─ Total earnings: ₱300,000
├─ 13th month bonus: ₱300,000 ÷ 12 = ₱25,000
├─ Tax on bonus: ₱0 (within ₱30K non-taxable limit)
└─ Payment: December 15
```

### Night Shift Premium - RA 6797
- ✅ 10 PM - 6 AM shift: 20% additional pay (minimum)
- ✅ Spa/massage industry: Applicable if night services offered
- ✅ ElSpa automatically flags night hours + adds 20% premium

**Example**:
```
Therapist working 8 PM - 6 AM (10-hour shift):
├─ 2 PM - 10 PM hours (8 hours): Regular ₱2,500/day = ₱20,000
├─ 10 PM - 6 AM hours (2 hours): Night shift ₱2,500 × 1.20 × 2 = ₱6,000
└─ Daily total: ₱26,000
```

### Sunday/Holiday Premium - RA 8369
- ✅ Sunday work: 30% additional pay (minimum)
- ✅ Regular holidays: 30% additional pay
- ✅ Special non-working holidays: 30% additional pay
- ✅ Double holiday: 30% additional pay
- ✅ ElSpa integrates Philippine holiday calendar

## 🏢 4. Healthcare/Nursing Home Regulations

### Department of Health (DOH) - Facility Licensing
- ✅ Nursing homes must be DOH-licensed
- ✅ ElSpa compliance: Audit trail for DOH inspections
- ✅ Staff credentials: Certification tracking + expiry alerts
- ✅ Record-keeping: 5-year retention for compliance

### DOLE (Department of Labor & Employment) - Labor Standards
- ✅ 8-hour working day (or as per contract)
- ✅ Rest day: Minimum 1 per week
- ✅ Overtime: 1.25x for first 4 hours, 1.30x thereafter
- ✅ ElSpa calculates automatically

**Overtime Rules**:
```
Regular 8-hour shift + 4 hours overtime:
├─ First 4 hours OT: Regular pay × 1.25
├─ Hours 5-8 OT: Regular pay × 1.30
└─ If on Sunday: Already paid 30% premium, then add 1.25-1.30x

Formula: Pay = Base hourly rate × hours × multiplier
```

### Employee Benefits (DOLE)
- ✅ SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG (mandatory)
- ✅ 5 days sick leave (if ≥1 month service)
- ✅ 5 days vacation leave (if ≥1 year service)
- ✅ Maternity/Paternity leave (per RA 8187)
- ✅ ElSpa tracks these automatically
