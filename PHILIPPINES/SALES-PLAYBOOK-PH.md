```
[Calendly link]

Best times: Tue-Thu 9-11 AM or 3-5 PM

Cheers,
[CEO Name]
```

### Week 1-2 Discovery Call Script

**Opening (1 min)**:
- "Thanks for taking time. I know you're busy."
- "Quick context: We noticed your customers ask for payroll. That's pain."
- "We built the API to solve this. 2-week integration, no maintenance."

**Problem Diagnosis (3 min)**:
- "How many customers ask about payroll?"
- "What do you tell them now?" (Probe)
- "What's your biggest payroll pain?"

**Solution Demo (5 min)**:
- Show: Live API call calculating payroll (2 min)
- Show: Tax withholding + premiums + 13th month bonus (1 min)
- Show: Partner integration example (2 min)

**Close (3 min)**:
- "Interested in a 7-day sandbox?" (Let them test)
- "If it works, we can move to POC?"
- "When could you start testing? Next Monday?"

### Week 2-3 POC Execution

**POC Scope** (minimal):
- [ ] Partner setup 50 test payroll calculations
- [ ] ElSpa API processes all correctly
- [ ] Partner integrates 1 customer's real data (test only)
- [ ] ElSpa provides calculation verification
- [ ] Partner signs off: "This works, let's go live"

**Timeline**: 1 week (Mon-Fri)

**Success Criteria**:
- [ ] ≥95% calculations match partner's expected values
- [ ] API response time <100ms
- [ ] Zero downtime during testing
- [ ] Partner NPS score > 8/10

### Week 3-4 Negotiation

**Negotiation Talking Points**:

**If Partner says**: "₱250/transaction is expensive"
**You say**: "Your current outsourcing is ₱500/transaction. We're 50% cheaper + fully integrated."

**If Partner says**: "We want white-label"
**You say**: "Sure. ₱75K/month white-label. You charge customers ₱400/transaction. Your margin: 87%."

**If Partner says**: "Can we do monthly instead of 12-month?"
**You say**: "Monthly possible, but ₱60K/month (vs ₱50K if 12-month). Volume commitment gives us certainty."

**If Partner says**: "Our legal team needs to review"
**You say**: "Of course. I'll send MSA template. Legal usually takes 2 weeks. Can we sign MOU meanwhile?"

### Success Metrics (Week 4)

- ✅ 1 POC contract signed
- ✅ API integration started
- ✅ ₱500K+ transaction volume in pipeline
- ✅ 2 additional partners in negotiation

**Day 1 (Go-live)**:
- [ ] Import facility data (caregivers, patients, rules)

- [ ] Help: "Can I show you how to [feature]?"

- "You've used ElSpa for 4 weeks. What do you think?"

**If they love it**:

## 🎓 Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "Too expensive"

**Root cause**: Customer doesn't see ROI

**Response**:
- "I hear you. Let me show the math."
- "Payroll errors = ₱10K-50K BIR fine. One error pays for 3 months service."
- "Scheduling efficiency = 1 less staff = ₱20K/month savings. That's 2x the cost."
- "So the software doesn't cost you money—it saves money."

### Objection 2: "We'll stick with Excel"

**Root cause**: Inertia, fear of change

**Response**:
- "Excel is familiar, I get it. But how many hours/month on payroll?"
- "Let's assume 4 hours/month at ₱500/hr = ₱2K. Yearly = ₱24K."
- "Plus payroll errors = ₱10K average/year."
- "Total cost of Excel = ₱34K/year. We're ₱12.5K/year. 37% of your current cost."

### Objection 3: "Your competitor is cheaper"

**Root cause**: Price sensitivity

**Response**:
- "Which competitor? [Listen]"
- "Wave costs ₱3,950 but doesn't handle Philippines tax."
- "Local systems cost ₱5-8K but no scheduling."
- "We're ₱4,950 with BOTH. Plus ₱12.5K for healthcare is less than 2 separate systems."

### Objection 4: "We don't have time to set up"

**Root cause**: Implementation concerns

**Response**:
- "I understand. That's why we handle setup."
- "Data import: 1 day. Staff training: 2 hours total. Live: 3 days."
- "We'll do most of the work. You just provide data + attend 1 training."
