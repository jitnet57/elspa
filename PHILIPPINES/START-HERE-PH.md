## 📋 What is ElSpa?

ElSpa is a unified automation platform for:

1. **Spa & Massage Businesses** (1-20 staff)
   - Booking + scheduling
   - Therapist shift management
   - Customer loyalty programs
   - Payroll + Philippine tax compliance
   - **Price**: ₱4,950/month

2. **Healthcare Facilities** (Nursing homes, hospitals)
   - Caregiver scheduling
   - Skill & certification tracking
   - Payroll with shift premiums
   - Compliance reporting
   - **Price**: ₱12,500/month

3. **HR/Payroll Platforms** (B2B partners)
   - White-label payroll API
   - Philippine tax engine (BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG)
   - REST API integration
   - **Model**: Per-transaction or flat licensing
