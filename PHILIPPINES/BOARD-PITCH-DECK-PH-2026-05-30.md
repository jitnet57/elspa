**Track C Tactics**:
1. **NPS-Based Segmentation**
   - High-risk (NPS < 0): Immediate outreach → discounts/promo
   - Neutral (NPS 0-30): AI recommendation → personalized packages
   - Loyal (NPS > 70): VIP loyalty → 30% higher re-visit rate

2. **AI Churn Prediction**
   - Increased visit gaps → 70% churn probability
   - Rising cancellations → 60% churn probability
   - Negative review sentiment → 50% churn probability

3. **Intervention Actions**
   - Auto alerts (SMS/WhatsApp/Email)
   - Limited-time offers (1-week validity)
   - Call center prioritization

**Week 1 Deliverable**:
- ✅ NPS data collection from 10+ existing customers
- ✅ AI churn prediction model (85%+ accuracy)
- ✅ Automated intervention workflow designed

**Speaker Notes (60 seconds)**:
- Track C is defense, not offense.
- We're currently losing ₱12M monthly to 40% churn—that's unsustainable.
- Reacquiring a lost customer costs 5x more than retaining them.
- Track C strategy is simple: find at-risk customers in data, deliver the right message at the right time.
- NPS + AI enable personalized interventions.
- Week 1 goal: collect NPS data from 10+ existing customers, validate AI model.
